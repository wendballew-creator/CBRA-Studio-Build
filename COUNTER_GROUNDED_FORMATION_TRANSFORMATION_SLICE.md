# Counter-Grounded Formation Transformation Slice

Date: 2026-08-18
Base commit: `3919a81`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose

Invited participants could produce raw, invitation-bound emissions from exact
arrival material, but callers still constructed complete
`RecordedProposalContribution` objects. Automatically accepting a schema
produced by the originating emission would restore the opaque semantic former.
A conventional reviewer applied afterward would restore after-the-fact
relationalization.

This slice transforms raw emission material into provisional local formation
inside the runtime while requiring a materially distinct counter-participant
to change the proposed cut before it can answer an invitation.

## Three apparatus relations

The transformation retains three distinct forms of participation:

1. **Originating participation** encounters the unresolved arrival and
   produces raw proposal-emission material.
2. **Transformation participation** encounters both the arrival and the exact
   emission and proposes cut-local distinctions. Its apparatus conditions,
   perceptibility, exclusions, and revision conditions remain explicit.
3. **Counter-participation** encounters the same arrival basis, adds grounded
   local structure, and reopens the proposed formation.

The transformation apparatus cannot disappear as a neutral parser. Its trace
basis must equal the arrival basis plus the proposal emission. It must differ
materially from both origination and checking participation.

## Operative transformation

`FormationMaterialTransformer` receives exact arrival and emission bytes and
may return only `ProposedFormationMaterial`:

- one provisional local cut;
- situated noticing material;
- position groundings; and
- generative differences.

It cannot supply origin/check authority or enact its cut.

`FormationCounterParticipant` receives the transformation request and
proposed material. Its `FormationChallengeMaterial` must include:

- materially distinct checking apparatus;
- new cut-local counter-positions;
- relations connecting those positions inside the proposed cut;
- grounding from every counter-position to proposed situated noticings;
- complications;
- additional exclusions; and
- reopening conditions.

Prose-only complication is rejected. Counter-participation must change the
cut's structural signature. Counter-position identifiers must be new and
local, counter-relations cannot escape the cut, and every counter-position
must be grounded.

## Runtime construction

Only after transformation and counter-participation validate does the runtime
construct the `SituatedCutProposal`, bind all three apparatus participations
to its noticings, construct `AccountableSituatedProposal` and
`RecordedProposalContribution`, and record the contribution against its
invitation.

The resulting contribution remains provisional. Plural enactment still
requires another materially different invitation-bound contribution. One
transformed proposal cannot enact a semantic field.

## Anti-authority boundary

Transformation success is not correctness. Counter-participation is not an
approval step. Additional exclusions, complications, and counter-structure do
not score or rank the proposal. The originating emission cannot supply its own
checking apparatus, and a prose-only reviewer cannot repair an already formed
answer.

Invalid transformation, hidden transformation apparatus, self-checking,
ungrounded counter-structure, or an out-of-cut relation leaves the invitation
unanswered and enacts no cut.

## Remaining external participation

The runtime now performs the transition from raw emission to a checked,
grounded contribution. Concrete transformer and counter-participant
implementations are still supplied externally. The fixtures demonstrate the
protocol but do not establish language understanding, adequate counter-cuts,
or independence between two implementations controlled by the same caller.

Transformation and challenge records remain runtime-local and do not yet
survive restart or recursively invalidate after later material disturbance.

## Bounded claim

Invitation-bound raw proposal material can now become a provisional situated
contribution inside the active runtime through explicit transformation and
grounded structural counter-participation. No originating emission, hidden
parser, prose-only review, or single transformed proposal can enact or certify
the semantic field.
