# Recursive Reconfiguration Checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-04
Pre-checkpoint head: `42111b7`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose of this checkpoint

This checkpoint consolidates the corrective sequence that followed the Case 2
paradigmatic failure. It does not certify CBRA as posthuman and does not claim
that a response runtime exists. It records which relations are operative,
which remain fixture-authored, where representation and humanism could
re-enter, and which invariants must hold before integration proceeds.

The architecture governs the code. Canonical commitments read together take
precedence over implementation convenience and passing tests. Code and tests
are evidence of bounded behavior only.

## Why the correction was necessary

The stopped application could receive language, persist sessions, call a
model, and produce responses, but its execution grammar still formed a
bounded individual, attributed failure, organized context around that person,
and released a corrective commitment. The first constitutive redesign renamed
objects while retaining a fixed pipeline and representational entities.

The correction therefore did not begin by repairing response wording. It
replaced the operative substrate beneath response formation.

## What is now operative

`cbra/reconfiguration/` currently enacts these bounded relations:

1. Stable transport inscriptions remain technical evidence and cannot declare
   semantic subjects, identities, intentions, failures, or roles.
2. Semantic positions exist only inside a local cut.
3. Dependencies and lineage are explicit, and disturbance recursively
   invalidates dependent cuts without deleting transport history.
4. Reconfiguration requires mechanically inspectable structural change.
5. Care-organized variation can redistribute a concentrated burden.
6. Materially distinct alternatives remain concurrent without ranking or a
   winner.
7. Diffractive comparison traces consequential difference and can reopen a
   local enabling formation.
8. Counter-cuts recompute arrangements without erasing displaced traces.
9. Recomputed formations can re-enter diffraction.
10. Oscillation remains evidence and can ground a local counter-cut without
    becoming failure, convergence, or iteration authority.
11. Observed effects return as situated confirmation, complication, or
    invalidation without becoming reward or truth labels.
12. Disturbing effects can ground further local revisions.
13. Complete feedback and revision histories remain inspectable.
14. Bounded continuity closures make present provenance validation local
    without replacing the preserved history.
15. Closure count, order, age, and renewal confer no maturity, adequacy,
    confidence, protection, or selection authority.

## What remains fixture-authored

The present substrate does not decide what a material arrival is or what it
means. Tests and callers still supply:

- local semantic positions and relations;
- cut boundaries, exclusions, and revision conditions;
- structural allocations and locally explicit harm constraints;
- which arrangements should be explored;
- consequential differences and enabling dependencies;
- observations and their disposition as confirming, complicating, or
  invalidating;
- counter-cuts and recomputed formations.

This is the decisive unfinished boundary. If a conventional caller or model
silently forms a sovereign user, a bounded event, an intention, a problem,
and a preferred answer before invoking this substrate, the new validators
would protect a representation produced elsewhere.

## Audit against representational and humanist re-entry

No operative path in `cbra/reconfiguration/` currently scores, ranks, chooses,
or declares a winner. No confidence, maturity, correctness, success, failure,
preference, convergence, or finality state is accumulated. Confirmation does
not protect a formation; later disturbance remains effective. Stable
transport identifiers do not become semantic identities. Public response
formation is absent rather than delegated to the legacy runtime.

The audit nevertheless leaves these open risks:

### External semantic formation

The largest risk is hidden upstream representation. Situated semantic
formation must make perceptible the apparatus conditions, enacted
distinctions, exclusions, burden and capacity relations, and reopening
conditions. A model proposal may participate as material but cannot originate
and certify its own cut.

### Measurements becoming objectives

Totals, maximum local load, occupied positions, and effect directions establish
structural difference against explicit local constraints. They are not values
to optimize and may not be aggregated into preference, fitness, or reward.

### Feedback becoming judgment

`CONFIRMS`, `COMPLICATES`, and `INVALIDATES` name situated correspondence or
disturbance. They do not mean true, partly correct, false, successful, failed,
approved, or rejected. Repetition creates history, not confidence.

### Source cuts becoming origins

`source_cut_id` is a local lineage and comparison relation. It is not the
original event, authoritative interpretation, or semantic center. A later cut
may reopen what the source cut enacted.

### Closures becoming certificates

A provenance closure commits to exact continuity material. It is necessary
for bounded validation of a feedback recomputation and insufficient for
participation, adequacy, protection, or truth. Persistence and caching must
not turn a valid digest into an acceptance certificate.

### Legacy runtime reactivation

The entity/stream application and constitutive experiment remain historical
engineering and falsification evidence. The active corpus boundary must
continue to fail closed. Integration must build around the new grammar rather
than translate it back into the old pipeline.

## Invariants at this checkpoint

- Structural measurements may be traced but not scored or compared to select
  an alternative.
- Harm constraints are explicit and local; the runtime may not infer a
  universal harm function.
- Feedback dispositions carry no numeric weight, terminal state, or truth
  judgment.
- Feedback history is append-only evidence; its length and order confer no
  authority.
- A continuity closure carries identifiers and an integrity commitment only.
- A closure cannot substitute for an active counter-cut, current trace,
  present field dependency, or consequential difference.
- `source_cut_id` confers lineage only and cannot select, protect, or certify a
  descendant.
- Rejected transactions leave substrate state unchanged.
- Passing invariants bounds a known failure; it never certifies the paradigm.

Executable probes for these negative capabilities live in
`cbra/test_architectural_authority_invariants.py` and complement the behavioral
tests for each operative relation.

## Correct next milestone

The next milestone is situated semantic formation, not another fixture-only
recursive generation. It must accept actual arrivals and produce provisional,
inspectable local cuts without treating a person, event, request, context, or
model interpretation as preformed. It must expose what participated, what was
excluded, and what would reopen the cut.

Only after that boundary is operative should formation activate the existing
structural operators. Public intervention, persistence, model participation,
and API/browser integration follow as separate milestones. The legacy
application remains blocked throughout.

## Claim boundary

The strongest claim available at this checkpoint is:

> CBRA now has a tested structural substrate in which local relations can
> vary, disturb, revise, return, and renew continuity without ranking,
> confidence, or cumulative authority. The formation of those relations from
> situated material and their enactment as public intervention remain
> unimplemented.

This checkpoint is a shared construction boundary, not a declaration that
CBRA is complete, correct, posthuman, or ready for deployment.
