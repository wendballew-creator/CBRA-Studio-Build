# Oscillation Counter-Cut Slice

## Bounded claim

This pass lets one exact unsupported structural-oscillation trace become
material for an explicitly proposed local counter-cut. Detection remains an
audit. It does not command the field to stop, settle, converge, restore a prior
state, or select an arrangement.

The new transaction requires:

- an audit recomputed exactly against recorded substrate history;
- a finding of unsupported departure and return, rather than persistence or
  materially responsive recurrence;
- one active enabling formation participating in the recurring state, the
  departure, and the return;
- transport evidence already operative in the returning witness;
- a counter-cut preserving lineage to the affected formation;
- local dependency anchoring in the formation being reopened; and
- a counter-cut whose topology is structurally distinct from that formation.

Only after all checks pass does one atomic substrate disturbance invalidate the
affected formation and its active dependents while enacting the proposed
counter-cut.

## Preserved history

`OscillationCounterCutTrace` retains the recurring, departing, and returning
witness identifiers, the exact responsiveness finding, the reopened enabling
formation, the counter-cut, and the mechanically derived substrate delta. The
invalidated cuts remain in substrate history.

The oscillation finding is evidence for why a local formation was reopened. It
is not evidence that the returning state is worse, that the departing state is
better, or that the new counter-cut should become final.

## Adversarial boundary

- Detection alone performs no mutation.
- Persistence and materially supported recurrence cannot authorize this path.
- A forged audit or changed departure record fails closed.
- An unrelated active cut cannot acquire counter-cut authority.
- A renamed copy of the reopened formation is not a counter-cut.
- Unrelated transport text cannot be substituted for operative return evidence.
- The same trace cannot repeatedly force revision after its local target has
  been invalidated.
- Rejected transactions leave substrate state unchanged.

No cycle count, duration, recency, revision depth, convergence threshold,
stopping rule, score, rank, winner, preference, chooser, model call, public
response, or persistence integration enters this slice.

## Remaining boundary

The substrate can now take up unsupported cycling as material and enact a
bounded counter-cut without converting detection into control. It still does
not determine whether recurrence should be interrupted, originate the
counter-cut from language, or trace how the newly enacted counter-cut affects
the wider competing field.

The next valid pass should return this oscillation-grounded counter-cut to the
competing arrangement field and recompute the formations that depended on the
cycle, preserving the possibility that the counter-cut itself may later be
disturbed.
