# Situated Formation Evidence Slice

Date: 2026-08-04
Base checkpoint: `b0a5974`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose

This slice begins situated semantic formation at the boundary between actual
arrivals and proposed local cuts. It does not claim that CBRA understands an
arrival or autonomously forms semantic positions. It makes the proposal's
material and apparatus conditions inspectable before the structural substrate
may enact it.

## Operative relation

`SituatedFormationBoundary` requires:

- exact nonempty byte spans from recorded transport traces;
- named apparatus participation through which each span became perceptible;
- an account of what that participation makes perceptible and obscures;
- unavailable or excluded material;
- explicit revision conditions for apparatus participation, noticings, and
  the proposed cut;
- grounding from every cut-local position to one or more situated noticings;
- exact equality between the cut's transport basis and its noticing evidence.

Only after these relations validate does the existing substrate atomically
enact the local cut. Failed formation leaves no cut or delta behind.

## What remains external

The caller still proposes the language used to describe a noticing, local
positions and relations, allocations, exclusions, and revision conditions.
This slice therefore displaces unmarked formation but does not yet solve
formation from actual language or other media. A later slice must make proposal
generation plural and inspectable without allowing a model to originate and
certify its own cut.

## Anti-authority boundary

The new records carry no score, rank, correctness, confidence, acceptance,
selection, maturity, or certification state. Exact evidence establishes
traceability, not truth. Apparatus participation establishes conditions of
perception, not an authoritative interpretation. A valid proposal remains a
provisional cut open to later disturbance.

## Bounded claim

An arrival-grounded local cut can now be enacted through an inspectable chain
of exact transport evidence, apparatus participation, situated noticing, and
position grounding. The runtime still does not generate or validate semantic
meaning independently of a proposal.
