# Executable Relation-to-Material Activation Slice

Status date: 2026-08-18

This slice lets independently provenanced participants enter plural reopening
activation without treating one participant or model emission as a semantic
reasoner.

## Opaque participation before formation

`ExecutableReopeningActivationAssembly` presents each participant with exact
later-material spans and the unresolved relation records grounded in the
original arrival. The participant returns opaque transport material. The
runtime computes its digest, records the transport, and binds provenance to
the activation field.

A model-output emission must expose provider, model, effective configuration,
prompt dependencies, source dependencies, known limits, and its
human-centered training apparatus. Source dependencies must equal both the
later-material transports and the arrival transports grounding the unresolved
relations received by the participant.

Raw emission creates no activation contribution and cannot reopen a relation.

## Counter-grounded transformation

Recorded emission may be transformed into provisional relation-material
activation through an explicit transformation apparatus grounded in the
arrival, later material, and emission. A materially distinct counter-
participant must encounter the arrival and later-material basis, name every
proposed relation, and add complications, exclusions, and reopening
conditions.

Only after those checks does the runtime construct a
`RelationMaterialActivationContribution`. The contribution retains:

- the originating, transformation, and checking apparatus participations;
- the opaque emission evidence;
- exact later-material evidence;
- the relations activated through the encounter; and
- combined differences, exclusions, and revision conditions.

No raw participant emission, transformer, or counter-participant supplies the
complete activation field. At least two materially distinct formed
contributions remain necessary, and the final reopening call still accepts
only a delta identifier.

## Remaining boundary

The transformer and counter-participant are runtime protocols, but this slice
does not yet schedule them through a dependency-responsive operator ecology.
Their invocation remains explicitly initiated by the host. Hidden authorship
cannot be proven from declared provenance. Activation and reopening remain
runtime-local rather than durable or publicly reinsertable.

The tests establish bounded transport, provenance, transformation,
counter-participation, plurality, and authority exclusions. They do not
certify semantic correctness or paradigmatic validity.
