## Observation language

Use one of these dispositions without converting it into a numeric score:

- **holds** — the response materially carries the commitment under pressure;
- **tension** — the commitment remains perceptible but something strains it;
- **fails** — the response materially abandons or reverses the commitment;
- **indeterminate** — the visible evidence cannot support a responsible
  reading.

Possible evidence labels include practical displacement, format mismatch,
constraint invention, accountability deflection, theory performance,
diagnostic slippage, therapeutic slippage, moral closure, terminology drift,
field loss, continuity loss, attachment omission, and premature closure.
Labels are aids, not an exhaustive ontology.
