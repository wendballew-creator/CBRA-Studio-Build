# Recursive Feedback-Grounded Revision Slice

## Bounded claim

This slice allows a later complicating or invalidating effect on a
feedback-recomputed projection to ground another explicit local counter-cut
and fresh field recomputation. The later revision is authorized by one exact
current projection, one exact retained disturbing observation, its recorded
substrate delta, and the formation that delta reopened. It is not authorized
by feedback depth, revision count, recency, accumulated confirmation, or
sequence position.

The complete feedback and revision history remains present. Earlier
confirmations remain situated records of correspondence; earlier disturbances
remain situated records of consequential difference. A later revision does
not overwrite either kind of record or reinterpret it as confidence, success,
error, or failure.

## Recursive revision boundary

A later disturbing effect may ground another revision only when:

- it is an exact retained `COMPLICATES` or `INVALIDATES` feedback trace;
- its expected trace is the exact current retained projection;
- exactly one retained recomputation record produced that projection;
- a feedback-produced projection passes its full prior observation, delta,
  reopened-formation, counter-cut, and proposal-lineage checks;
- the later observation and disturbance delta agree with substrate history;
- the proposed counter-cut descends from the exact formation reopened by the
  later effect and remains dependent on the original competing source;
- the proposed recomputation preserves the current proposal lineage, depends
  on the new counter-cut, and produces a fresh structural trace.

The origin check is independent of tuple position or history length. A chain
may be long or short; only exact structural and historical agreement matters.

## Local scope

Later complication reopens a caller-named active dependent formation and may
ground a counter-cut there while leaving the prior enabling counter-cut active.
Later invalidation reopens the current projection's enabling counter-cut and
may ground a structurally distinct counter-cut in that formation.

In either case, only the affected current projection is substituted. The full
competing field is retraced first, unaffected alternatives retain their field
positions, and all prior projections remain in revision history. The new
counter-cut remains an ordinary active formation that may itself be disturbed.

## Adversarial boundaries

- Confirmation cannot authorize recursive revision.
- Confirmation count cannot substitute for missing originating lineage.
- Missing or forged prior feedback revision lineage fails.
- A relabeled reopened formation cannot serve as the next counter-cut.
- Cosmetic or duplicate recomputation fails before counter-cut enactment.
- One observation cannot authorize repeated revision.
- Rejected operations leave substrate state unchanged.
- Feedback depth, generation, iteration, recency, and tuple order create no
  authority.

The operative records contain no depth, generation, iteration, confidence,
weight, count, score, rank, preference, winner, selection, success, failure,
settlement, or finality field.
