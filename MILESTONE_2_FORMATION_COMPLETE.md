# Milestone 2: Situated Formation Complete

Status date: 2026-08-18

Milestone 2 is complete at this bounded repository checkpoint.

This is a claim about operative situated-formation behavior, not a claim that
CBRA is paradigmatically valid, production-ready, persistent, publicly
intervening, or behaviorally verified.

## Exit conditions

| Exit condition | Operative evidence |
|---|---|
| Formation from actual text, document, tool, and material arrivals | One mixed-arrival field records `text/plain`, `application/pdf`, `tool-result`, and `material-state` transports; exact spans enter unresolved relation evidence; plural participant emissions are transformed and counter-grounded; concurrent cuts are enacted with all four transports as their basis. |
| Inspectable proposal-generating participation | Unresolved relations activate materially distinct invitations. Participants receive exact arrival bytes and apparatus limits. Emissions retain provider/model or other provenance, remain visible as transport, and cannot answer invitations by themselves. |
| Counter-cuts and structural probes during formation | A proposal cannot be recorded until materially distinct counter-participation adds new local positions, relations, groundings, complications, exclusions, and reopening conditions. The runtime compares pre- and post-challenge cut structure and rejects prose-only or structurally unchanged checking. |
| Apparatus descriptions can themselves be revised | Plural reopening invalidates the old invitation field. Revised apparatus descriptions are then recorded separately against invalidated invitations, must encounter both original relation evidence and later material, and must materially change their relational descriptions. An incomplete or cosmetically rebound revision field cannot form a successor assembly. |
| Recursive invalidation of unresolved relations and invitations | Later material must enter through plural inspectable activation. Reopening invalidates every invitation and provisional proposal in the shared formation field and recursively invalidates enacted dependent cuts while preserving transport history. |
| No opaque model functioning as semantic former | Model calls produce opaque transports only. Runtime transformation and materially distinct counter-participation are both required before a contribution exists. Multiple contributions are required before enactment, and final enactment accepts no caller-supplied proposal or activation field. |

## Operative sequence without a fixed pipeline

The milestone does not install a universal former–reviewer chain. Relations
open participation invitations; invitations may remain unanswered; emissions
may arrive in any order; raw emission has no semantic effect; counter-
participation changes provisional structure before recording; later material
may invalidate any dependent formation; and successor apparatus revisions are
recorded separately before a new field can form.

These are dependency conditions, not mandatory global stages. There is no
winner, score, confidence, accepted interpretation, maturity state, or final
semantic subject.

## Why this closes rather than extends the record layer

The closing slice adds no new record schema. It adds behavior to the existing
formation assembly:

- mixed transport channels participate in one exact evidence basis;
- reopening evidence changes the relation basis of a successor field;
- apparatus revisions are recorded one invitation at a time;
- incomplete revision cannot reactivate formation; and
- the final successor-field operation derives its invitations from those
  recorded revisions.

The milestone is therefore closed on enacted formation behavior rather than
on vocabulary or schema completeness.

## Boundary after Milestone 2

The next milestone begins outside situated formation. Remaining work includes
durable persistence of the recursive grammar, public intervention formation
and reinsertion, architecture-native API/browser integration, live provider
participation under the new grammar, and behavioral falsification against the
canonical probes.

Passing tests establishes only the bounded mechanics named above. It cannot
establish that no hidden human-centered formation remains or that a declared
apparatus account exhausts what participated.
