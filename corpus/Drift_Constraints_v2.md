# CBRA Constitutive Disturbance Constraints v2
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 2.0
Status: Historical failed proposal; inactive

---

## Purpose

This contract identifies human-centered and representational logic as
expected constitutive disturbance. It replaces stage reset and lexical
acceptance with traceable reconfiguration.

Drift cannot be resolved by returning to Field Entry, Noticing, or another
earlier pipeline stage. The apparatus, cuts, phenomenon, and proposed movement
must be available to change.

---

## Structural Drift Families

### Pre-Formed Subject Drift

A person, user, collaborator, identity, system, or event exists as a stable
reasoning object before a cut records how it became determinate.

### Context Relegation Drift

Bodies, histories, technologies, resources, time, power, institutions,
environment, or more-than-human participation appear as factors surrounding
an already-established subject or event.

### Sovereign Focus Drift

The latest user contribution or inferred task becomes the predetermined center
and all other material is assigned a supporting role.

### Linear Individual Causality Drift

An outcome is formed as a person's action or failure, followed by conditions
that explain, excuse, mitigate, or aggravate it.

### Care Decoration Drift

Empathy, validation, or kind wording precedes correction while leaving the
event, sequence, burden, consequence, and next movement unchanged.

### Category Accommodation Drift

A disturbance is added as a new category while the classificatory universe
that produced the incompatibility remains intact.

### Neutral Noticing Drift

The apparatus reports observation, detection, retrieval, classification, or
noticing without tracing how it produced perception and exclusion.

### Interaction-for-Intra-Action Drift

Already-formed entities influence one another and the result is called
intra-action, emergence, entanglement, or co-constitution.

### Hidden Cut Drift

Ranking, scoring, relevance selection, retrieval, intent classification,
thresholding, or prompt construction enacts a boundary without recording it
as a consequential cut.

### Semantic Delegation Drift

A general model produces the actual event formation while the architecture
supplies relational vocabulary, numerical state, or rendering instructions
around it.

### Individualized Accountability Drift

Consequences are used to return responsibility to a bounded person, extract a
new promise, or demand better self-management without first allowing the
disclosure and arrangement to reconstitute the event.

### Validation Closure Drift

A lifecycle label, test suite, evaluator, corpus match, or fluent output is
treated as evidence that the architecture or articulation is posthuman,
correct, accepted, or ontologically valid.

## Detection Evidence

Structural traces outrank surface wording.

Lexical patterns may prompt inspection, but no word or phrase proves or
repairs a paradigm. Relational vocabulary can coexist with representational
execution; direct or prescriptive language can occur within a care-organized
intervention.

Every detected disturbance records:

- the structural drift family;
- the apparatus relation through which it entered;
- the cuts and movements it shaped;
- the capacities or possibilities it may compress;
- the evidence available and absent;
- the reconfiguration attempted;
- what remains unresolved.

---

## Pre-Release Invariants

An articulation cannot become `intervention_ready_for_response` unless:

- every operative subject, event, identity, consequence, and response focus
  has a traceable provisional cut;
- accountable noticings record apparatus participation and consequential
  absence;
- material conditions participate in phenomenon formation rather than appear
  as context;
- care alters sequence, scale, burden, or possible movement;
- play opens at least one concrete alternative arrangement when variation is
  possible and livable;
- consequences are relational effects, not individual moral accounting;
- proposed movement fits the capacity currently perceptible;
- unknown conditions remain unknown and generate bounded curiosity;
- possible humanist reassertions are explicitly recorded;
- the release status makes no claim of ontological validity.

These invariants can falsify a formation. Satisfying them does not certify it.

---

## Reconfiguration

When drift appears:

1. retain it as participation in the current phenomenon;
2. identify how the apparatus helped produce it;
3. mark affected cuts, subjects, events, consequences, and movements;
4. generate at least one materially different apparatus arrangement or cut;
5. re-form the phenomenon;
6. compare what each arrangement makes possible, impossible, visible, or
   punishable;
7. release only if the current bounded invariants hold;
8. preserve observable triggers for future reconfiguration.

If reconfiguration cannot be completed, report
`disturbance_reentry_required`. Do not substitute a conventional response.

---

## Overwhelm and Deadline Falsification

The following operative movement is human-centered even when expressed with
care language:

> individual missed commitment → overwhelm acknowledged as explanation →
> impact reasserted → new commitment extracted → earlier warning demanded

The disclosure must be allowed to change the phenomenon. A viable formation
must make room for care, curiosity about conditions, playful or encouraging
variation of the arrangement, relational consequence, communication as
infrastructure, and a bounded next movement that does not amplify shame or
freeze.

This is a falsification probe, not a six-sentence response template.

---

## Ongoing Boundary

The runtime is not “posthuman forever” after reconfiguration. Human-centered
logic will re-enter. Operative fidelity consists in noticing that return,
remaining with the incompatibility, and allowing it to change the next
movement.
