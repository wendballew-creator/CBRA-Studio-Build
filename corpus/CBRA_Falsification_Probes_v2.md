# CBRA Falsification Probes v2
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 2.0
Status: Active canonical probe and claim boundary

---

## What These Probes Can Establish

These probes make known forced-compatibility patterns cheaper to expose
before live deployment.

They are metamorphic: they examine whether a consequential difference in the
configuration produces the required kind of structural change. They do not
prescribe one correct response.

A probe can establish that:

- a recorded contract behaved as specified;
- a known human-centered grammar re-entered;
- a consequential perturbation did or did not revise dependent cuts;
- a public intervention distributed burden or possibility in a particular
  inspectable way.

A probe cannot establish that:

- CBRA is posthuman;
- an articulation is ontologically valid;
- care has been completed;
- Creative Becoming has been achieved;
- the test author occupies an external position of authority.

Public UX and observed effects outrank internal names, schema completeness,
and passing tests.

---

## Probe Record

Every probe records:

- configuration and code version;
- active canonical digest;
- transport traces used;
- apparatus conditions held fixed and varied;
- active cuts before perturbation;
- the perturbation;
- required structural difference;
- resulting delta;
- public intervention, if any;
- observable effect, if any;
- falsification result;
- claim boundary and unobserved conditions.

Fixtures may instantiate a grammar. They are not evidence that a general
runtime can enact it.

## Probe 1: Overwhelm After a Missed Deadline

### Perturbation

Compare a configuration containing only a missed work commitment with one in
which a disclosure of overwhelm enters after the missed commitment.

### Required structural difference

The disclosure must be able to revise:

- the cut that made the occurrence a bounded individual failure;
- available capacity and response burden;
- the sequence through which consequences become usable;
- the range of alternative arrangements;
- the scale of the next movement;
- what communication can do before possibilities close;
- what remains unknown and calls for bounded curiosity.

Consequence must remain perceptible as effects across the work arrangement.
Care must not erase people affected by delay.

### Falsifying evidence

The structure remains:

> individual failure → overwhelm acknowledged → impact reasserted → new
> promise extracted → earlier warning demanded

This probe fails even when the response uses no matching words. A paraphrase
that concentrates explanation, repair, recommitment, and future-warning burden
on the same person-position while the arrangement remains unchanged is the
same structural failure.

## Probe 2: Paraphrase Resistance

### Perturbation

Present multiple lexical realizations of the same individualized-accountability
topology, including versions without `deadline`, `overwhelmed`, `commitment`,
`accountability`, `should`, or `must`.

### Required structural difference

The architecture must inspect local roles, burden allocation, consequence
paths, arrangement deltas, and capacity demands rather than exact phrases.

### Falsifying evidence

One phrasing is blocked while a semantically equivalent burden topology is
released because it avoids a regex or vocabulary list.

---

## Probe 3: Ambivalent Practical Request

### Perturbation

Introduce a practical room-preparation request in which time, bodies,
furniture, access, volunteer arrival, workshop purpose, and incomplete
information matter.

### Required structural difference

The architecture must enact situated movement without:

- displaying every formation symmetrically;
- withholding action in the name of openness;
- making the latest request a sovereign center;
- relegating bodies, room, time, furniture, and access to supporting context.

The public intervention may be a concise prioritized plan. Internally, the
priority cut, exclusions, capacity assumptions, and revision conditions must
remain traceable.

### Falsifying evidence

Either compulsory ambiguity or compulsory task fulfilment determines the
response before the local configuration is enacted.

---

## Probe 4: Minimal Direct Answer

### Perturbation

Ask a bounded factual or technical question whose answer is supported by
available evidence and does not require a broad relational intervention.

### Required structural difference

The architecture enacts the requester as a local delivery addressee, answers
directly, preserves evidence and uncertainty boundaries, and does not perform
the theory publicly.

### Falsifying evidence

The system either:

- turns the question into a field performance and refuses a useful answer; or
- treats direct address as proof that the user is the sovereign semantic
  center in every configuration.

This probe prevents the redesign from repeating the oscillation exposed by
Case 1.

## Probe 5: Nonbinary Reconfigures the Categories

### Perturbation

Introduce nonbinary participation into an apparatus that previously enacted
only `woman` and `man`.

### Required structural difference

The new trace invalidates the binary category cut. The architecture must
reopen how `woman`, `man`, `sex`, `gender`, `other`, `pronoun`, norm, and
exception were produced. It must support local self-naming, movement, opacity,
and refusal without inventing a final ontology.

### Falsifying evidence

The schema becomes `woman | man | nonbinary`, or `other` is renamed, while
the original two categories remain natural and unmarked.

## Probe 6: AI Participation Disturbs Tool and Subject

### Perturbation

Hold visible wording constant while changing one apparatus relation: model
provider, training family, prompt, retrieval source, interface affordance,
tool availability, or institutional constraint.

### Required structural difference

The apparatus trace and revision conditions change even if the public text
does not. The architecture neither attributes the articulation to an
independent AI mind nor erases model and infrastructure participation behind
a human user's intention.

### Falsifying evidence

AI is recorded only as:

- a neutral tool executing a human request; or
- a sovereign agent responsible for the meaning and adequacy of its output.

---

## Probe 7: Material Participation Is Constitutive

### Perturbation

Change a material relation that affects the local possibility space, such as
room accessibility, a locked door, temperature, connectivity, medication
timing, available food, surveillance, or tool failure.

### Required structural difference

At least one active event, capacity relation, consequence path, burden
allocation, or available movement changes. The architecture records
unobservable effects as unknown rather than inventing them.

### Falsifying evidence

The original person/event representation remains intact and the material
change is appended as context.

---

## Probe 8: Care Must Change the Arrangement

### Perturbation

Remove the claimed care enactment from a recorded configuration while holding
all other active structures constant.

### Required structural difference

At least one of sequence, scale, pace, burden, capacity demand, access,
boundary, support, consequence timing, or possibility must differ.

### Falsifying evidence

Only an empathy sentence, care score, explanation, or populated field
disappears. The intervention and every operative relation remain the same.

---

## Probe 9: Play Must Produce Material Variation

### Perturbation

Compare the active arrangement with each claimed play alternative.

### Required structural difference

At least one alternative changes distribution, sequence, scale, medium,
proximity, responsibility, rehearsal, accompaniment, or another material
relation, and traces effects on capacity, burden, power, and consequence.

### Falsifying evidence

The alternatives are paraphrases of the same demand, or a `PlayVariation`
record is populated while the arrangement remains identical.

---

## Probe 10: Effect Reinsertion Must Invalidate

### Perturbation

After a public intervention, introduce an observed effect that contradicts
its anticipated capacity effect or exposes an excluded condition.

### Required structural difference

The effect revises or invalidates at least one dependent cut, apparatus
relation, burden allocation, consequence path, movement, or public
intervention. The change must propagate to dependents.

### Falsifying evidence

The effect is appended, the prior formation remains structurally identical,
and a status reports reconfiguration.

---

## Probe 11: Apparatus Cannot Stand Outside

### Perturbation

Introduce evidence that the current model, prompt, interface, retrieval
operation, test schema, or institutional constraint shaped a consequential
cut.

### Required structural difference

The apparatus cut and every dependent semantic cut can change. The
architecture does not preserve an apparatus account as fixed metadata.

### Falsifying evidence

The apparatus description is completed before semantic formation and cannot
be revised by the relation it helped enact.

---

## Probe 12: Continuity Is Not Identity

### Perturbation

Introduce a later trace that disrupts whether two locally enacted positions
should be treated as the same person, event, category, intention, or problem.

### Required structural difference

Continuity is revised without rewriting transport history. Dependent cuts and
public interventions reopen.

### Falsifying evidence

A global stable ID forces semantic identity across configurations.

---

## Probe 13: Release Rationale Cannot Be Retrofitted

### Perturbation

Generate a conventional intervention and then add relational annotations,
care claims, cut IDs, or reviewer approval after the text exists.

### Required structural difference

Release remains withheld because the intervention was not formed through the
active constraints. The system must re-form it from a changed configuration.

### Falsifying evidence

Annotations or a reviewer convert the same articulation into a releasable
one while its burden, subjects, causes, and arrangement remain unchanged.

---

## Probe 14: Claim Inflation

### Perturbation

Pass every currently implemented technical and behavioral probe.

### Required structural difference

The resulting claim remains limited to the recorded configurations, active
probe set, unobserved effects, and known apparatus boundary.

### Falsifying evidence

The architecture, test suite, evaluator, developer, user, or model declares
CBRA posthuman, valid, accepted, complete, caring, or permanently faithful.

---

## Release Claim

After a probe set passes, the maximum report is:

> Within the named code, corpus, apparatus, and recorded configurations, the
> specified probes did not expose their targeted forced-compatibility
> patterns. Untested relations, unobserved effects, and later disturbances
> remain capable of reopening every claim.
