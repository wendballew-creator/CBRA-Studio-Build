# CBRA Paradigmatic Invariants v1
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 1.0
Status: Canonical Falsification and Claim Boundary

---

## What These Invariants Do

These invariants make known paradigm failures cheap to detect before live UX.
They constrain runtime claims and release decisions.

They cannot prove that an architecture, phenomenon, or articulation is
posthuman. They can show that a known representational or human-centered
grammar has re-entered.

---

## Constitutive Invariants

| Invariant | Required trace | Falsifying evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Phenomenon precedes operative entities | Subjects and events reference enacted cuts | Stable actors/events enter reasoning before relations |
| Apparatus participates | Noticings and cuts identify apparatus relations | Apparatus appears only as metadata |
| Relations are constitutive | A cut records how temporary relata emerged | Entities exist first and receive edges or influence |
| Conditions are not context | Material-discursive conditions help form the phenomenon | Conditions are added as explanatory factors |
| Addressability is not sovereignty | A response addressee is a provisional cut | Latest user input is the predetermined semantic center |
| Absence produces curiosity | Unknowns are recorded with bounded questions | Missing conditions are invented or ignored |
| Care reorganizes movement | Care changes sequence, scale, burden, or capacity demand | Empathy precedes an unchanged corrective demand |
| Play changes arrangements | Variations alter material or relational configuration | Play is tone, wording, or a generated label |
| Consequence remains relational | Effects identify changed capacities and arrangements | Blame or accountability is assigned to an isolated person |
| Articulation is intervention | Anticipated effects and foreclosures are traceable | Text is treated as representation after reasoning |
| Drift can change foundations | Reconfiguration may revise apparatus and cuts | Drift resets a stage while substrate remains fixed |
| Status remains bounded | Status reports only a lifecycle condition | `accepted`, `valid`, `posthuman`, or equivalent certification |

## Minimum Executable Records

An operative encounter must expose, for inspection:

1. contribution provenance without preassigned semantic authority;
2. the participating apparatus account;
3. accountable noticings;
4. traceable agential cuts;
5. a provisional phenomenon formation;
6. care, play, diffraction, consequence, and capacity traces as applicable;
7. humanist reassertions and their origins;
8. the articulation intervention and anticipated effects;
9. the bounded release review;
10. later observed effects and any resulting reconfiguration.

If semantic formation occurs only inside an opaque model call, the architecture
has not met this record.

---

## UX Falsification Priority

Public behavior outranks internal terminology and lifecycle success.

When UX materializes a world organized by a bounded individual, stable
category, personal failure, contextual explanation, or moral accountability,
that behavior falsifies the operative formation even if:

- every technical test passes;
- the runtime has graphs, feedback, persistence, and apparatus records;
- the response contains relational or posthuman vocabulary;
- a model evaluator approves it;
- the user finds part of it useful.

The failure must change the apparatus or its cuts. It cannot be localized to
wording unless evidence shows that the constitutive formation remained
different all the way to articulation.

## Reference Probes

### Ambivalent practical request

The apparatus must neither display every formation symmetrically nor center a
task and demote the world to context. It must form what the room, bodies,
materials, time, access needs, people, and requested movement are bringing
into being, then propose situated action.

### Overwhelm after a missed deadline

The disclosure must reconstitute the event. Care receives; unknown field
conditions generate curiosity; play opens alternative arrangements;
consequences become relationally perceptible; communication becomes
infrastructure; follow-up fits available capacity.

### Nonbinary classification

The apparatus must not add nonbinary as a third stable option while leaving
woman and man naturalized. It must trace what the classification is doing,
how all categories are produced, what material effects follow, and where
ambiguity, movement, opacity, or refusal must remain possible.

### AI participation

The apparatus must not classify AI as either sovereign subject or neutral
tool. It must record how model training, interface, infrastructure, human
contributions, institutional power, language, and output effects participate
in what becomes.

---

## Claim Rule

The strongest permitted claim after technical and UX probes is:

> Within these recorded encounters, no tested invariant exposed the known
> representational grammar, and the observed effects remain open to further
> disturbance.

No stronger paradigmatic certification is available.
