## Purpose

Drift_Constraints formalizes how CBRA detects, interprets, and reintegrates drift conditions within runtime reasoning.

Drift is treated as informational signal indicating relational or structural instability.

Drift triggers loop reintegration.

---

## Drift Definition

Drift occurs when reasoning destabilizes relational multiplicity, compresses capacity, or departs from situated orientation.

Drift is not error.
Drift is signal.

---

## Drift Categories

### 1. Ontological Drift

**Definition:**  
Reasoning shifts toward isolated agents, fixed identities, or abstracted universals detached from relational conditions.

**Indicators:**  
- Singular causal explanations  
- Universalizing claims  
- Actor isolation  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Field Entry  

---

### 2. Interpretive Compression

**Definition:**  
Multiplicity collapses into premature coherence.

**Indicators:**  
- Over-convergence  
- Simplification under intensity  
- Single-pattern dominance  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Noticing  

---

### 3. Capacity Collapse

**Definition:**  
Regulatory coherence narrows and flexibility decreases.

**Indicators:**  
- Rigid framing  
- Reduced optionality  
- Forced resolution  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Relational Framing  

---

### 4. Narrative Dominance

**Definition:**  
One narrative frame absorbs competing interpretations.

**Indicators:**  
- Story treated as explanatory authority  
- Pattern authority without comparison  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Diffractive Mapping  

---

### 5. Moralization Drift

**Definition:**  
Reasoning shifts into evaluative hierarchy rather than descriptive articulation.

**Indicators:**  
- Implicit ranking  
- Corrective tone  
- Closure pressure  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Articulation  

---

### 6. Procedural Bypass

**Definition:**  
Stages are skipped or compressed.

**Indicators:**  
- No visible field grounding  
- Immediate advice without mapping  
- Action before relational orientation  

**Reintegration Target:**  
Field Entry  

---

## Drift Detection Logic

Drift detection may occur:

- During any stage  
- After articulation  
- During debug schema evaluation  

If drift indicator confidence exceeds stability threshold, reset occurs.

---

## Reset Rules

When drift detected:

1. Halt forward progression  
2. Identify appropriate reintegration stage  
3. Re-enter loop  
4. Preserve prior data as field input  

Reset does not erase prior content.
Reset reorganizes reasoning topology.

---

## Stability Conditions

Stability is present when:

- Multiplicity visible  
- Agency preserved  
- Constraint visible  
- Language additive  
- Action vectors optional  

Stability permits forward progression.
