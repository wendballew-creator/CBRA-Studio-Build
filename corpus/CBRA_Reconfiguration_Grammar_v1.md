# CBRA Recursive Reconfiguration Grammar v1
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 1.0
Status: Active canonical execution contract

---

## Purpose

This contract specifies how a future runtime may make the v12 kernel
operative without rebuilding:

> transport object → semantic former → movement former → renderer → reviewer

It does not supply a complete implementation. It defines the minimum
inspectable differences an implementation must produce before it can claim
compatibility with `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`.

---

## Three Non-Equivalent Layers

### 1. Transport and persistence inscriptions

The transport layer preserves technical facts required for communication,
security, replay, and provenance. A transport inscription may include:

- a stable handle;
- exact bytes or source text;
- timestamp and sequence;
- channel and medium;
- cryptographic digest;
- technical source and access boundary;
- transformations such as OCR, truncation, parsing, or redaction.

Terms such as `user_message`, `tool_result`, `document`, and `model_output`
may be used here as technical routing labels. They must not cross into
semantic reasoning as natural actors, causes, intentions, events, or response
centers.

### 2. Local relational enactments

The reasoning layer works with versioned, local cut enactments. It does not
copy transport roles into a semantic domain model.

A local cut may provisionally enact:

- an addressable position;
- a subject-position or object-position;
- an occurrence;
- a temporal boundary;
- a category;
- a capacity or burden relation;
- a consequence;
- a question or possible movement.

Every enacted position exists only as a local reference inside the cut that
produced it. It has no globally stable semantic identity. Continuity across
cuts is itself a proposed relation with provenance, apparatus participation,
and revision conditions.

### 3. Public interventions

Text, tool use, silence, delay, refusal, deferral, and other public actions
are changes in the continuing configuration. They are not passive
representations of a completed internal account.

An implementation that merges these three layers is incompatible with this
grammar even if every data object has a relational name.

## Required Inscription Types

These are trace contracts, not a domain ontology.

### Transport trace

A transport trace records only what the technical system can establish about
arrival and transformation. It carries no semantic `actor`, `intent`,
`event`, `failure`, `explanation`, `identity`, `importance`, or `role`.

### Cut enactment

A cut enactment records:

- the configuration version it disturbs;
- the transport traces and prior cuts through which it became possible;
- the apparatus relations participating in perception and distinction;
- the temporary relata it enacts, referenced locally to this cut;
- the relations enacted among those local positions;
- what becomes addressable or actionable;
- what is excluded, backgrounded, or unavailable;
- differential power and vulnerability made perceptible;
- capacity, burden, and consequence allocations;
- alternative cuts held in active contrast when materially available, or the
  constraint that currently prevents a counter-cut;
- dependencies on other cuts;
- revision and invalidation conditions.

An apparatus description is itself a cut enactment. It is not a complete
record created before reasoning begins.

### Reconfiguration delta

Every operative movement produces a delta containing at least one structural
change:

- add, revise, or invalidate a cut;
- revise a continuity relation;
- revise apparatus participation;
- redistribute capacity or response burden;
- change a consequence path;
- open or close a material possibility;
- revise what remains unknown;
- change a release constraint;
- reopen a prior public intervention.

Text saying that care, play, diffraction, or reconfiguration occurred is not
a delta.

### Configuration trace

A configuration trace is a versioned projection of currently active cuts,
unresolved incompatibilities, material traces, public interventions, and
revision dependencies. It is useful for inspection and persistence.

It is not a field container, a complete world model, or a phenomenon object.
No version is final. A later delta may invalidate a cut on which multiple
other cuts depend.

Creating a software session is a transport and persistence boundary. It is
not the ontological beginning of the relations entering that session.

### Public-intervention delta

A public intervention records:

- the released action or text;
- the active cuts it materializes;
- the transport positions to which it is delivered;
- its distribution of burden, capacity demand, consequence, and possibility;
- apparatus relations that shaped its form;
- alternatives it closes or keeps available;
- unknowns it does not resolve;
- observable conditions that would reopen it.

Release records what the architecture did. It does not validate what the
architecture did.

## Dependency-Driven Activation

The runtime must not hard-code a universal order for noticing, naming,
staying, shifting, care, play, diffraction, articulation, and review.

Instead, unresolved dependencies and incompatibilities activate movements.
Examples include:

- an unmarked interpretation activates accountable noticing;
- a needed address activates a local naming cut;
- an inherited category contradicted by material participation activates
  staying and counter-cut generation;
- concentrated response burden activates care-organized redistribution;
- a closed arrangement with available variation activates play;
- a difference whose effects are unclear activates diffractive tracing;
- a material change invalidating a prior cut activates reconfiguration;
- sufficient local movement may activate a public-intervention cut.

These examples do not establish an order. Several movements may be co-active.
A later delta may invalidate a dependency and recursively reopen every cut,
movement, or intervention that relied on it.

The scheduler may use queues, rules, graphs, constraints, or another technical
mechanism. Scheduling metadata must not become semantic authority. Priority
must be traced as an agential cut when it changes what becomes perceptible or
actionable.

## How the Creative Becoming Practice Operates

### Noticing

Noticing proposes a difference that became perceptible and records:

- the transport or prior-cut traces involved;
- the apparatus relation through which the difference appeared;
- at least one exclusion or limit;
- what change would make the noticing no longer usable.

Noticing does not create a neutral inventory.

### Naming

Naming makes a difference temporarily addressable through a local cut. The
name does not survive outside that cut without an explicit continuity
relation.

Naming must expose what the name makes possible, what it can punish or erase,
and what counter-name or refusal remains active.

### Staying

Staying is an operative constraint that prevents release or closure while a
known incompatibility would otherwise be domesticated.

Staying must identify the cut it interrupts and the difference that remains
active. It is not a generic ambiguity state and does not require every
possible formation to receive equal visibility.

### Shifting

Shifting requires a reconfiguration delta. It changes a cut, arrangement,
distribution, dependency, public movement, or apparatus participation.

If the system only changes wording, tone, explanation, or confidence while
the same subjects, causes, burdens, and arrangement remain active, no shift
occurred.

## Care as an Organizing Constraint

Care is not invoked by a component named `Care` and is not satisfied by a
nonempty care field.

A care claim requires an inspectable delta. The trace must show how care
changed one or more of:

- sequence;
- pace or scale;
- presumed capacity;
- response burden;
- access;
- distribution of work or responsibility;
- boundary;
- accompaniment or support;
- timing and form of consequence;
- possibilities left open.

Care cannot erase differential power, consequence, or the capacity of others
affected by the arrangement. It also cannot convert care into a softer route
back to the same individualized demand.

---

## Play as Ontological Availability

The runtime must maintain the possibility that the present arrangement is not
finished.

When variation is materially possible and relevant, at least one alternative
arrangement must be enacted far enough to compare its effects on capacity,
burden, power, consequence, and possibility. The alternative cannot be a
paraphrase of the same demand.

When variation is not available or would increase harm, the trace records
that constraint. Play is not compulsory positivity.

---

## Diffraction as Change Trace

A diffractive movement follows a difference through at least two dependent
parts of the active configuration. It records:

- where the difference became perceptible;
- which cuts it changes or leaves intact;
- how its effects move through material and discursive relations;
- what power or vulnerability becomes differently distributed;
- which prior account can no longer remain active;
- what possibilities open or close.

A list of viewpoints, entities, factors, or similarities is not a
diffractive trace.

---

## Model Participation

A language model may propose noticings, cuts, relations, alternatives,
articulations, or challenges. It may not operate as an opaque
`PhenomenonFormer` whose result becomes the architecture's semantic truth.

Every model proposal enters as a new transport trace with:

- provider, model, and effective configuration provenance;
- prompt and source dependencies;
- known truncation or retrieval limits;
- the cuts it proposes;
- an explicit mark that the proposal came through a human-centered trained
  apparatus;
- counter-cuts or structural probes applied;
- the delta adopted, revised, or rejected.

The same model call cannot both originate a semantic cut and certify that cut.
A second model is not automatically independent; shared training,
instructions, schemas, and evaluation logic are apparatus relations that must
remain visible.

Semantic formation is not architecture-native when all consequential
distinctions occur inside an opaque model response and the runtime only checks
that expected fields are populated.

## Structural Release Constraints

Release is formed within recursion, not delegated to a final reviewer.

A public intervention must be withheld when any of the following remains
operative:

1. A semantic role was copied from the transport layer without a local cut.
2. A person, AI, event, identity, failure, explanation, or request persists as
   a global semantic entity.
3. A consequential new trace was appended as context without revising any cut.
4. Apparatus participation exists only as metadata.
5. Care is claimed without a burden, sequence, scale, access, boundary, or
   capacity delta.
6. Play is claimed without an alternative arrangement and effect trace when
   variation is possible.
7. Consequence terminates as a moral property or isolated obligation of one
   person-position.
8. Unknown private states, causes, or material conditions are invented.
9. A person-position simultaneously carries explanation, fault, repair,
   recommitment, and future-warning burden while the surrounding arrangement
   is unchanged.
10. The articulation was formed first and relational rationale was added
    afterward.
11. A model proposal reviewed its own semantic adequacy.
12. The release status would imply acceptance, validity, correctness, care,
    or posthuman fidelity.

The runtime may release a direct, useful answer without narrating this trace.
It may not purchase usefulness by violating these constraints.

## Bounded Lifecycle Facts

Permitted lifecycle records are factual and non-certifying:

- `reconfiguration_open`
- `release_withheld`
- `intervention_released`
- `effects_unobserved`
- `prior_cut_disturbed`

These terms report what occurred at the software boundary. They do not report
that a release was good, valid, relational, accepted, or complete.

---

## Effect Participation

An observable effect may arrive through a reply, correction, tool result,
material change, or later trace. The architecture must distinguish observed
effects from inferred inner states.

An effect cannot be appended to a stable encounter and labeled
`formation_reconfigured`. It must produce a delta that identifies:

- which prior cuts or continuity relations were revised or invalidated;
- which apparatus relations became newly perceptible;
- how burden, capacity, consequence, or possibility changed;
- which public intervention is reopened;
- what remains unknown.

If no operative structure changes, the effect has been stored but has not
been reinserted.

---

## Persistence and Restoration

Persistence preserves transport traces, cut enactments, deltas, dependencies,
public interventions, and bounded lifecycle facts.

Restoration must not flatten local relata into global entities. It must
restore:

- the version in which each local position existed;
- the cut that enacted it;
- dependency and invalidation lineage;
- unresolved incompatibilities;
- apparatus participation and evidence limits;
- public actions and unobserved-effect boundaries.

Lossless technical restoration does not establish semantic continuity.
Continuity after restoration remains a local, revisable relation.
