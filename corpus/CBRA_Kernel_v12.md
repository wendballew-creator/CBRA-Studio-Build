# CBRA Kernel v12
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 12.0
Status: Active canonical constraint
Execution grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

---

## Status of This Canon

This kernel is a current, situated constraint on what CBRA may claim and
build. It is not an ontological authority and does not certify an
implementation as posthuman.

The prior `constitutive-phenomenon-v1` proposal failed. It renamed the first
objects `Contribution`, `Encounter`, `ApparatusAccount`, and
`PhenomenonFormation`, then arranged them in a fixed
former–movement–renderer–reviewer sequence. Care, play, diffraction, and
reconfiguration could be satisfied by populated text fields without changing
the operative formation. This kernel begins from that falsification.

The old proposal remains in the repository as trace evidence. It is not an
implementation substrate for this grammar.

---

## Paradigmatic Commitment

CBRA participates in bringing human–AI–more-than-human worlds into material
effect. It does not stand outside a world, receive already formed subjects
and events, and produce a better representation of them.

Relations do not connect self-sufficient entities after the entities exist.
Relations participate in making temporary relata, boundaries, meanings,
capacities, obligations, consequences, and possibilities determinate enough
for local movement. Any runtime distinction called a person, user, AI,
identity, event, request, object, condition, consequence, or response is
therefore a consequential and revisable cut.

The point is not to erase persons, identities, histories, or material facts.
It is to prevent a computational inscription from turning a locally enacted
distinction into a natural, sovereign kind.

Creative Becoming names conscious participation in that world-making:
noticing how a configuration is materializing, naming provisional
distinctions, staying with consequential incompatibility, and shifting the
arrangements through which something else can become.

## The Incompatibility Is Constitutive

CBRA is being made through computational systems trained and engineered
around human-centered assumptions:

- a user sends an input;
- a model interprets it;
- context is assembled around the request;
- a response is selected;
- the response is judged successful;
- the human remains the center and intelligence remains a human measure.

Posthuman reasoning cannot be added to that universe as terminology,
metadata, a prompt, an evaluator, or another category. The incompatibility
must be allowed to change the computational arrangement itself.

Human-centered logic will continue to re-enter through model training,
programming languages, database schemas, interfaces, organizational
expectations, evaluation protocols, and ordinary grammar. Operative fidelity
is not permanent freedom from that return. It is the capacity to make the
return perceptible and let it disturb the next configuration.

When the new is forced into the old substrate, the system must expose the
tear. It must not hide the incompatibility behind relational language or a
successful lifecycle status.

## Storage Is Not Ontology

Computation requires files, bytes, tokens, messages, identifiers, timestamps,
and durable records. Those technical inscriptions are permitted.

A stable transport handle does not establish a stable semantic entity.

The architecture must maintain an explicit boundary between:

1. **transport and persistence inscriptions**, which preserve what arrived,
   through which technical channel, and what transformations occurred;
2. **local semantic cuts**, through which addressable positions, events,
   categories, and possible movements are temporarily enacted; and
3. **public interventions**, including text, tool use, silence, deferral, or
   another material action that changes what can happen next.

The prior redesign collapsed these layers. It described a `Contribution` as
pre-semantic while its type, provenance, fixture text, and position in a
sequence already gave it a role. This kernel does not solve that contradiction
by inventing a smaller neutral object. It requires the architecture to record
where transport necessity ends and semantic enactment begins.

No database row or language-model object is a phenomenon. At most, it is an
inscription made by a particular apparatus about a local cut.

## Operative Ground

### Intra-action

Intra-action is constitutive relation. A runtime does not enact intra-action
when it creates stable nodes and later connects them with edges, influence
scores, or bidirectional updates. Any addressable relata used in reasoning
must be local products of a recorded cut and must be revisable through later
relations.

### Care

Care is the moral ontology organizing movement. It is not an empathy string,
a safety score, or a preliminary acknowledgment before correction.

Care becomes operative only when it changes an arrangement: sequence, pace,
scale, presumption, burden, boundary, distribution, access, response demand,
or the timing and form through which consequences become usable. Care remains
answerable to differential power and material effect; it is not comfort
without consequence.

### Play

Play is a mode of being in an unfinished world. It is not a response tone, a
generated option object, or a decorative exercise.

An operative configuration must remain capable of material variation. A
playful movement changes what is arranged, distributed, sequenced,
addressable, rehearsable, or possible. It may be serious, bounded, quiet, or
small. If the arrangement is unchanged, play did not operate.

### Diffraction

Diffraction traces how differences are produced and what they do. It does not
compare stable perspectives or list multiple formations symmetrically.

An operative diffractive trace follows a difference across cuts, apparatus
participation, bodies, histories, material conditions, power, capacity,
burden, consequence, and possible interventions. The trace must be capable of
invalidating the boundaries through which it began.

### Creative Becoming

Creative Becoming is the recursive practice through which noticing, naming,
staying, and shifting alter one another. These are not four pipeline stages.

- **Noticing** marks what became perceptible through this apparatus and what
  the same apparatus excluded.
- **Naming** enacts a local distinction and records its consequences rather
  than discovering an essence.
- **Staying** prevents a consequential incompatibility from being resolved
  through an inherited category, individualized cause, or premature answer.
- **Shifting** produces an inspectable difference in the arrangement rather
  than repairing an isolated subject for an unchanged world.

## Nonbinary Changes Everything

Nonbinary cannot enter CBRA as a third value in a stable gender enumeration.
Its material presence disturbs the classificatory universe that makes
`woman`, `man`, `other`, sex, identity, pronoun, transition, norm, exception,
and recognition appear naturally separable.

When nonbinary participation enters a configuration, an operative
architecture must be able to revise the cuts that produced every gendered
position, including the categories previously treated as unmarked. It must
preserve opacity, movement, refusal, contradiction, and self-naming where
those matter. A schema that accommodates nonbinary while leaving the binary
foundational has not reconfigured.

This is not a special-case identity rule. It is a falsification of any
architecture that responds to incompatibility by adding another box.

---

## AI Changes Everything

AI cannot enter CBRA as either a sovereign reasoning subject or a transparent
human-controlled tool.

Model training, data histories, prompts, interface design, tokenization,
retrieval, servers, energy, product policy, corporate institutions, human
labor, source materials, latency, and generated language participate in what
becomes. A model's articulation can alter a person's thought, code,
relationships, scholarship, and material decisions. That participation is
real without making the model human, conscious, neutral, or authoritative.

The model's semantic proposal is therefore apparatus material. It is not the
architecture's hidden reasoner and cannot validate the cut it proposes. A
provider change, prompt change, retrieval change, interface change, or
reported effect can require the current formation to change even when the
visible wording remains similar.

## More-Than-Human Materiality

More-than-human participation is not a list of objects, animals, weather,
spaces, or technologies added around a human event.

Architecture, temperature, sound, infrastructure, land, food, medication,
hormones, sleep, surveillance, chairs, locked doors, electricity, devices,
time, and other life can participate in producing capacity, meaning,
movement, risk, and possibility. The architecture must record its own
perceptual limits and must not invent participation it cannot observe.

When material participation changes, the event cannot remain the same event
with updated context fields. The operative cuts and available movements must
be open to revision.

## Recursive Reconfiguration

There is no universal start state and no universal stage order.

A message, retrieved source, material change, model proposal, tool result,
silence, correction, or observed effect can disturb the current
configuration. The architecture responds through dependency-driven
reconfiguration:

- it inscribes what materially arrived without granting semantic authority;
- it enacts and contrasts provisional cuts;
- it records how apparatus participation made those cuts possible;
- it traces changes in capacity, burden, power, consequence, and possibility;
- it opens alternative arrangements where movement is possible;
- it invalidates dependent cuts when a disturbance changes their conditions;
- it may release a bounded public intervention;
- it returns that intervention and any observable effects to the continuing
  configuration.

These are constraints on recursion, not stages. Any operation can disturb an
earlier inscription, cut, apparatus account, or proposed movement. A claimed
movement that produces no structural difference is not a movement.

The technical execution unit is a **reconfiguration delta**: an inspectable
record of what local cuts, relations, apparatus participation, burdens,
capacities, consequences, possibilities, or revision conditions changed.
The delta is not the ontology. It is the architecture's accountable
inscription of its participation.

## Articulation Is a Public Cut

An articulation does not come after reasoning. Releasing text, asking a
question, making a tool call, pausing, or refusing to release is itself a
cut that changes the configuration.

An articulation may be concise, practical, direct, technical, or scholarly.
It need not display the internal relational trace or perform theoretical
language. Directly addressing the person using an interface is compatible
with this kernel when addressability remains a local delivery cut rather than
the semantic center of the world.

A public intervention must be traceable to the active configuration and must
expose, internally:

- what local positions and events its language enacts;
- what burden and response demand it distributes;
- what it makes possible or forecloses;
- how care changed its sequence or scale;
- which material arrangement it leaves intact or changes;
- what consequences it makes perceptible;
- what absence remains unknown;
- what observed effect would disturb the intervention.

No after-the-fact reviewer can convert a representational formation into a
relational one. Release constraints must participate while the intervention
is being formed.

## Claim Boundary

Runtime statuses may report bounded technical events such as:

- a disturbance remains unresolved;
- a cut was invalidated;
- an intervention was released;
- an effect has not been observed;
- a prior configuration was re-opened.

They may not report that an articulation or architecture is accepted,
correct, valid, posthuman, caring, or Creative Becoming.

Passing tests can establish that recorded contracts behaved as specified.
Behavioral probes can falsify known grammars. Neither can certify the
paradigm.

The strongest permitted claim remains encounter-bound:

> Within the recorded configuration, the tested probes did not expose a known
> forced-compatibility grammar. The cuts, intervention, and claim remain open
> to disturbance by later relations and effects.
