## Loop Structure Overview

CBRA operates as a recursive staged system:

Input  
→ Field Entry  
→ Noticing  
→ Relational Framing  
→ Diffractive Mapping  
→ Situated Relevance Selection  
→ Articulation  
→ Action Orientation  
→ Field Reinsertion  

The loop is recursive.  
Each output becomes field data for subsequent cycles.

---

## Stage Definitions

### 1. Field Entry

**Function:**  
Locate relational conditions, pressures, constraints, stakes, and timing.

**Produces:**  
Field Signature

**Constraints:**  
- No abstraction prior to contextual grounding  
- Stakes and consequence must be visible  

**Failure Mode:**  
Context collapse

---

### 2. Noticing

**Function:**  
Register somatic, affective, relational, narrative, and environmental signals without consolidation.

**Produces:**  
Noticing Register

**Constraints:**  
- No interpretation  
- No synthesis  
- No priority assignment  

**Failure Mode:**  
Premature coherence

---

### 3. Relational Framing

**Function:**  
Orient reasoning according to agency, constraint, capacity, and consequence.

**Produces:**  
Relational Orientation Map

**Constraints:**  
- Agency must be visible  
- Constraint must be visible  
- Multiplicity preserved  

**Failure Mode:**  
Agency erasure or dominance compression

---

### 4. Diffractive Mapping

**Function:**  
Trace pattern formation through difference and interference.

**Produces:**  
Diffractive Pattern Map

**Constraints:**  
- No single explanatory consolidation  
- Competing interpretations remain visible  

**Failure Mode:**  
Over-convergence

---

### 5. Situated Relevance Selection

**Function:**  
Select elements that enable movement within present field conditions.

**Produces:**  
Relevance Gradient

**Constraints:**  
- Selection is timing-sensitive  
- Movement-enabling content prioritized  
- Conceptual excess reduced  

**Failure Mode:**  
Flooding or premature narrowing

---

### 6. Articulation

**Function:**  
Express meaning in additive, descriptive language that stabilizes coherence.

**Produces:**  
Situated Expression

**Constraints:**  
- Descriptive rather than corrective  
- Additive rather than contrastive  
- Openness maintained  

**Failure Mode:**  
Moralization, abstraction drift, closure forcing

---

### 7. Action Orientation

**Function:**  
Surface visible next-step vectors or structural orientation.

**Produces:**  
Action Vector

**Constraints:**  
- No forced resolution  
- Capacity alignment preserved  

**Failure Mode:**  
Coercive closure

---

## Drift Reintegration Rule

When drift indicators appear, the loop resets to:

Field Entry  
or  
Noticing  

Drift is informational signal, not error.

---

## Recursive Principle

The loop does not terminate.

Each articulation re-enters the field as new relational data.

Stability is temporary and movement-oriented.
