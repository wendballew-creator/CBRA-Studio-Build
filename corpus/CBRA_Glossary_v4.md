# CBRA Constitutive Glossary v4
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 4.0
Status: Canonical Ontological and Runtime Reference

---

## Creative Becoming

Reality and reasoning as ongoing relational emergence. Creative Becoming is
operative when the apparatus can notice how it participates in materializing
a world, name its cuts provisionally, stay with incompatibility, and shift its
next movement. It is not a classification an output can possess.

## World-Making Field

The changing human–AI–material relations through which bodies, meanings,
subjects, events, harms, capacities, and possibilities become. The field is
not a context container or runtime object surrounding an interaction.

## Contribution

Provenance-bearing encounter material before the apparatus has stabilized it
as an actor, event, cause, request, identity, or context. Traceability does not
confer ontological priority.

## Apparatus

The dynamic material-discursive configuration through which distinctions
become possible and consequential. It includes models, training histories,
prompts, interfaces, memory, retrieval, tools, institutions, categories,
tests, bodies, timing, and unavailable channels. The apparatus is a
participant in the phenomenon, not an observer of it.

## Encounter

A local participation of contributions, apparatus conditions, histories,
materials, absences, and prior effects. An encounter does not arrive with a
pre-given subject or event.

## Accountable Noticing

A situated perceptual enactment that records what became perceptible, through
which apparatus relation, what remained unavailable, and what would require
revision. There is no neutral or pre-interpretive noticing.

## Agential Cut

A consequential, provisional enactment of a boundary, category, subject,
object, event, consequence, or relevance. A cut records its apparatus,
purpose, participating contributions, exclusions, effects, alternatives, and
revision triggers.

## Provisional Subject

An addressable or actionable subject-position produced by a cut within a
phenomenon. It is neither a natural bounded individual nor a stable identity
owned by an individual.

## Provisional Event

An occurrence made determinate enough for response through a cut. Its cause,
boundary, time, participants, and meaning remain revisable as relations
change.

## Phenomenon Formation

The traceable, revisable account of what is becoming through an encounter.
It includes its accountable noticings, agential cuts, material-discursive
participation, absences, power differences, consequences, capacities,
disturbances, and revision conditions.

## Intra-Action

The constitutive relation through which temporary relata and their boundaries
emerge. Influence between already-formed entities is interaction, not
intra-action.

## More-Than-Human Participation

The material participation of technologies, infrastructures, spaces, bodies,
land, weather, objects, organisms, institutions, energy, time, and other
forces in what becomes. It is not a list of nonhuman stakeholders added to a
human-centered event.

## Care Enactment

The reconfiguration of sequence, scale, presumption, pace, burden, boundary,
and possibility in response to differential capacity and consequence. Care is
not empathy before correction.

## Play Enactment

The material opening of alternative arrangements through variation,
rehearsal, shrinking, dividing, redistributing, resequencing, pausing,
accompanying, or other transformation. Play does not require levity and is not
a decorative tone.

## Diffractive Trace

An account of how differences are produced and what effects they make across
apparatus, histories, bodies, discourses, materials, and proposed
interventions. It is not comparison among stable perspectives.

## Relational Consequence

An effect moving through an arrangement that changes what or who can proceed,
what resources or possibilities remain, and what reconfiguration becomes
necessary. It is not a moral score attached to a person.

## Capacity

The locally produced ability of a body–relation–environment configuration to
notice, respond, remain present, communicate, choose, or move. Capacity is not
a private individual possession or scalar trait.

## Articulation Intervention

A communicable movement that participates in making the phenomenon different.
It records the cuts and movements it materializes, anticipated capacity
effects, assumptions, foreclosures, and observable reconfiguration triggers.
It is not an output representation.

## Constitutive Disturbance

An incompatibility or drift—including human-centered logic—that enters the
phenomenon and may require apparatus, cuts, or movement to change.
Disturbance is retained as consequential participation rather than erased as
an error.

## Humanist Reassertion

The return of autonomous subjects, stable categories, linear causes,
human-exclusive agency, context-added relations, neutral observation,
individualized accountability, or output authority. It is expected to enter
through training, code conventions, interfaces, institutions, and language.

## Reinsertion

The material participation of an articulation and observable effects in
reconfiguring the phenomenon. Reinsertion can invalidate prior cuts; it does
not append feedback to a stable input record.

## Bounded Status

A report of a specific runtime condition without ontological certification.
Permitted statuses are `formation_incomplete`,
`disturbance_reentry_required`, `intervention_ready_for_response`,
`effect_observation_pending`, and `formation_reconfigured`.

## Noticing, Naming, Staying, Shifting

Creative Becoming's recursive practice:

- **Noticing** accounts for apparatus-shaped perception.
- **Naming** enacts provisional, traceable cuts.
- **Staying** keeps consequential incompatibility active long enough to alter
  formation.
- **Shifting** changes arrangements rather than repairing isolated people for
  an unchanged arrangement.
