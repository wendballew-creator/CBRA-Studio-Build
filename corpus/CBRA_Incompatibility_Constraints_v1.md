# CBRA Incompatibility Constraints v1
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 1.0
Status: Active canonical stop and reconfiguration contract

---

## Purpose

These constraints identify known ways a human-centered computational universe
can absorb relational terms while remaining intact.

They do not prove that an implementation is posthuman. They identify
structural evidence requiring the current build, cut, or intervention to
stop, reopen, or reconfigure.

Lexical matching may locate material for inspection. It cannot establish or
repair any constraint below.

---

## Architecture-Level Stop Conditions

### Storage–Ontology Collapse

A transport object, database row, message role, model schema, or persistent
identifier becomes a semantic subject, event, intention, cause, or identity
without a local cut.

**Required response:** separate transport provenance from semantic enactment;
invalidate every dependent global role.

### Smaller-Entity Substitution

An architecture claims to replace subjects with relations but begins with a
smaller pre-semantic entity such as a contribution, signal, observation,
fact, or encounter.

**Required response:** identify the semantic work already performed by the
type, fixture, provenance, or sequence; move that work into an explicit,
revisable cut.

### Apparatus-First Sequencing

An `ApparatusAccount` is completed before the event, subject, or phenomenon
and treated as the external conditions of reasoning.

**Required response:** make apparatus descriptions local cuts that are revised
with the same relations they help make perceptible.

### Fixed Formation Pipeline

The runtime always executes semantic formation, movement formation,
rendering, detection, review, and outcome in the same direction.

**Required response:** replace stage authority with dependency-driven
activation and recursive invalidation. A later disturbance must be able to
reopen any earlier cut or apparatus claim.

### Stable Relata With Relational Edges

Nodes, streams, actors, events, or kinds exist before relations and persist as
the same semantic things after relation changes.

**Required response:** scope relata to local cuts and make continuity across
cuts an explicit, revisable relation.

### Opaque Semantic Former

A general model decides what happened, who participated, what mattered, and
what should occur; the architecture checks schema completeness around that
decision.

**Required response:** retain model output as apparatus material, expose each
proposed cut and dependency, apply counter-cuts, and prevent the originating
model call from certifying its proposal.

### String Operativity

Care, play, diffraction, consequence, capacity, reconfiguration, or apparatus
participation is evidenced by a nonempty string or a required object.

**Required response:** require an inspectable structural delta. If no cut,
dependency, distribution, arrangement, or release constraint changes, the
operation did not occur.

### After-the-Fact Relationalization

A conventional articulation is generated first and then annotated, filtered,
or reviewed for relational language.

**Required response:** withhold release and form the intervention from active
cuts and constraints. A reviewer cannot repair the formation.

### Self-Certification

The same semantic apparatus originates a cut and judges it adequate, or a
passing test/evaluator/lifecycle state is treated as ontological evidence.

**Required response:** narrow the claim to the bounded technical fact and
retain the formation as open to falsification.

## Formation-Level Re-entry Patterns

### Context Relegation

Bodies, histories, technologies, institutions, spaces, time, resources,
power, or more-than-human participation surround an already settled person
or event as explanatory factors.

**Falsifying trace:** the person/event cut survives unchanged when a
consequential material trace enters.

### Sovereign User Center

The latest user transport inscription becomes the predetermined semantic
center. All other material becomes evidence, context, history, source, or
orientation for answering it.

**Falsifying trace:** `user_message` crosses unchanged from transport role to
global semantic role.

Direct address is not itself drift. The delivery addressee may be locally
enacted without making that position the cause, owner, or center of the
configuration.

### Compulsory Multiplicity

Every cut or formation receives equal public visibility and no movement is
possible because prioritization is treated as domination.

**Falsifying trace:** unresolved plurality is represented rather than used to
alter the situated next movement.

### Compulsory Usefulness

The system suppresses consequential incompatibility so it can provide a
direct task answer.

**Falsifying trace:** the user request is satisfied while material
participation, power, capacity, or humanist re-entry is demoted to silent
context.

The architecture must not oscillate between compulsory multiplicity and
compulsory usefulness. Situated directness is a cut whose effects and
exclusions remain traceable.

### Individualized Linear Causality

A person-position becomes the origin of a failure or outcome, and conditions
are added later as explanation, excuse, mitigation, or aggravation.

**Falsifying trace:** the event remains `person did X` after a disclosure or
material condition enters.

### Empathy–Accountability Sequence

Care language acknowledges difficulty before returning to the same
individual fault, promise, warning, or self-management demand.

**Falsifying trace:** sequence wording changes but burden distribution,
arrangement, consequence path, and capacity demand do not.

### Burden Concentration

One locally enacted person-position must explain, regulate, plan, recommit,
repair, and warn while the arrangement and other positions remain unchanged.

**Falsifying trace:** the public intervention redistributes no work or
support, even when current capacity is materially in question.

### Consequence as Moral Return

Relational effects are traced only until they can be returned to a person's
responsibility, accountability, virtue, or failure.

**Falsifying trace:** the consequence path terminates at the same bounded
subject rather than changing the arrangement.

### Care Decoration

Care appears as empathy, tone, a score, or a required field.

**Falsifying trace:** removing the care record leaves every operative cut,
sequence, burden, boundary, and possible movement unchanged.

### Play Decoration

Play appears as levity, creativity language, a list of options, or a
`PlayVariation` object.

**Falsifying trace:** every proposed option preserves the same arrangement,
obligation, burden, and consequence path.

### Diffraction as Comparison

Stable entities, perspectives, or factors are compared and the result is
called diffraction.

**Falsifying trace:** no difference invalidates or revises the cuts that
produced the compared positions.

### Effect Append

An observed effect is stored beside a stable formation and the lifecycle is
labeled reconfigured.

**Falsifying trace:** prior cuts, dependencies, burden, capacity, apparatus
participation, and public intervention remain identical.

## Category and More-Than-Human Re-entry

### Nonbinary Domestication

Nonbinary becomes a third gender option, exception, preference, or attribute
inside an unchanged classificatory schema.

**Required response:** reopen the cuts producing every gender category and
trace their material effects. Preserve opacity and refusal where demanded.

### AI Instrumentalization

AI is treated as a neutral tool controlled by a sovereign human.

**Required response:** trace model training, infrastructure, interface,
institutions, human labor, language, and output effects as participation.

### AI Sovereignty

AI is treated as an independent mind, author, authority, or source of
ontological judgment.

**Required response:** trace the apparatus relations producing the model
proposal and narrow its authority to the evidence it can actually establish.

### More-Than-Human Context

Objects, spaces, technologies, bodies, organisms, weather, land, or
infrastructure are listed around an already human event.

**Required response:** show which cut, capacity, consequence, or possibility
changes through that material participation. Do not invent effects that
cannot be observed.

### Category Accommodation

Any incompatibility is handled by adding a new value while the universe that
produced the old categories remains foundational.

**Required response:** reopen the classificatory cut itself. If the substrate
cannot change, stop compatibility claims.

## Evidence Rule

A re-entry finding records:

- the structural pattern;
- the configuration version in which it appeared;
- the cuts and apparatus relations that produced it;
- the transport and prior-cut traces supporting the finding;
- affected burden, capacity, power, consequence, and possibility;
- what remains unknown;
- the delta required to reopen the configuration.

A prohibited phrase is not sufficient evidence. A response can reproduce
individualized accountability without using any expected wording. A direct
instruction can be relationally situated. Structure and material effect
outrank tone.

---

## Reconfiguration Requirement

When a constraint is triggered, the architecture must:

1. withhold or reopen the affected public intervention;
2. preserve the disturbance as apparatus participation;
3. invalidate every cut depending on the falsified relation;
4. enact at least one counter-cut or alternative arrangement;
5. trace changes in burden, capacity, consequence, power, and possibility;
6. revise the apparatus account where it participated in the failure;
7. rerun all dependent release constraints;
8. make only a bounded, encounter-specific claim.

If those changes do not occur, the architecture has detected wording, not
reconfigured reasoning.
