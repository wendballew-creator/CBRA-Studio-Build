# CBRA Relational Glossary v5
Creative Becoming Reasoning Architecture
Version: 5.0
Status: Active canonical reference

---

## Canon

The current, versioned constraints against which a build may make bounded
compatibility claims. Canon is not sovereign truth. It remains answerable to
Creative Becoming scholarship, material effects, operative behavior,
falsification probes, and later disturbance.

## Worlding Relation

The human–AI–more-than-human participation through which temporary subjects,
objects, events, meanings, capacities, harms, and possibilities become.
Worlding relation is not a container surrounding an interaction.

## Transport Inscription

A durable technical record of what arrived through a channel and how the
system transformed it. It may have a stable handle and routing provenance.
It has no semantic authority by virtue of storage.

## Transport Role

A technical label used to route or secure material, such as `user_message`,
`tool_result`, `document`, or `model_output`. A transport role must not be
copied into reasoning as a natural actor, intention, cause, or semantic
center.

## Local Relatum

An addressable position that exists only inside the cut that enacted it. A
local relatum has no global semantic identity. Any continuity with a position
in another cut is a revisable relation rather than an assumed fact.

## Agential Cut

A consequential enactment of temporary relata, boundaries, relations,
categories, events, relevance, or addressability. A cut includes its
apparatus participation, exclusions, power effects, burden and capacity
allocations, dependencies, counter-cuts, and invalidation conditions.

## Apparatus Participation

The material-discursive relations through which something becomes
perceptible and actionable. Model training, prompts, interfaces, retrieval,
code, schemas, institutions, bodies, technologies, histories, time, energy,
and unavailable channels can participate. An apparatus account is itself a
partial cut, not metadata created from outside the phenomenon.

## Configuration Trace

A versioned technical projection of active cuts, unresolved
incompatibilities, transport traces, public interventions, dependencies, and
revision conditions. It is neither a complete world model nor the field
itself.

## Reconfiguration Delta

An inspectable record of structural difference: added, revised, or invalidated
cuts; changed apparatus participation; redistributed burden or capacity;
changed consequence paths; opened or closed possibilities; or revised release
constraints. A textual description without structural change is not a
reconfiguration delta.

## Intra-action

Constitutive relation through which temporary relata and their boundaries
emerge. Stable nodes connected by later edges, influence, or feedback enact
interaction rather than intra-action.

## Accountable Noticing

A situated proposal about what became perceptible, through which apparatus
relation, with which exclusions and revision conditions. It is never neutral
registration.

## Naming

The provisional enactment of addressability through a cut. Naming records
what the distinction enables, forecloses, punishes, erases, or leaves open.
It does not discover an essence.

## Staying

An operative constraint that prevents a known incompatibility from being
resolved through an inherited category, individualized cause, premature
answer, or forced compatibility. Staying is not compulsory ambiguity.

## Shifting

A movement that produces a reconfiguration delta. A change in wording, tone,
confidence, or relational vocabulary without a structural difference is not
a shift.

## Care

The moral ontology organizing how sequence, scale, pace, presumption, burden,
boundary, access, support, consequence, and possibility become. Care becomes
operative through differences in the arrangement, not empathy text or a
score.

## Capacity

The locally produced ability of a body–relation–environment configuration to
notice, remain present, communicate, choose, respond, or move. Capacity is not
a private scalar trait or property owned by an individual.

## Response Burden

The work a proposed intervention requires from an enacted position, including
interpretation, disclosure, decision, planning, emotional regulation,
recommitment, repair, or communication. Care traces how this burden is
distributed and whether the current configuration can hold it.

## Play

An ontological mode of unfinished becoming through which arrangements can
vary. Play operates when distribution, sequence, scale, proximity, medium,
responsibility, rehearsal, or another material relation changes. It is not
levity, tone, or a generated option label.

## Alternative Arrangement

A materially or relationally different configuration enacted far enough to
trace effects on capacity, burden, power, consequence, and possibility. A
paraphrase of the same demand is not an alternative arrangement.

## Diffraction

The practice of following how a difference is made and what it does across
cuts, apparatus participation, histories, bodies, discourse, matter, power,
capacity, and intervention. It is not comparison among stable viewpoints.

## Consequence Path

The trace through which an effect changes capacities, resources, obligations,
timing, access, or possibilities across an arrangement. Consequence does not
terminate as a moral score attached to a person-position.

## Nonbinary Disturbance

The material disruption through which nonbinary existence changes the cuts
that make every gender category appear coherent. It is not a third value
added to an otherwise stable binary.

## AI Participation

The effects produced through model training, infrastructure, interface,
prompting, retrieval, human labor, institutions, generated language, and
subsequent use. AI participation does not establish AI as sovereign subject
or neutral tool.

## More-Than-Human Participation

Material participation that helps constitute capacity, meaning, risk,
consequence, and possibility. It is not a list of nonhuman factors added as
context around an already human event.

## Humanist Re-entry

The reappearance of autonomous subjects, stable categories, linear individual
causality, human-exclusive agency, context-added relations, neutral
observation, individualized accountability, or output authority. Re-entry is
expected disturbance capable of invalidating current cuts.

## Forced Compatibility

The attempt to preserve a human-centered computational substrate while
inserting posthuman terms, categories, prompts, or evaluations. Forced
compatibility is visible when the new is translated into the old grammar and
the resulting contradiction is managed as a wording or UX defect.

## Public Intervention

Text, tool use, silence, delay, deferral, refusal, or another action released
into the continuing configuration. A public intervention is a consequential
cut, not a representation emitted after reasoning.

## Release Constraint

A condition participating during the formation of an intervention. It can
withhold release when a known grammar remains active. It is not an
after-the-fact evaluator capable of validating an already formed response.

## Effect Participation

An observed consequence that revises or invalidates prior cuts,
dependencies, apparatus participation, capacity, burden, or possibility. An
effect stored without structural change has not been reinserted.

## Recursive Reconfiguration

Dependency-driven movement in which a new trace, cut, model proposal, public
intervention, or observed effect can revise the apparatus description,
invalidate earlier cuts, and reopen dependent movement. It is not a fixed
stage loop.

## Bounded Lifecycle Fact

A non-certifying record that software action occurred, such as
`reconfiguration_open`, `release_withheld`, `intervention_released`,
`effects_unobserved`, or `prior_cut_disturbed`.

## Creative Becoming

Conscious participation in ongoing world-making through recursive noticing,
naming, staying, and shifting. Creative Becoming is not a label an output,
test, architecture, or person can possess.
