The agential cut is not a choice made by a human subject; it is a boundary-making practice that produces the subject and object. In correctional education, the 'cut' often stabilizes the identity of 'prisoner' and 'teacher.' CBRA seeks to make these cuts permeable, allowing for a 'creative becoming' where roles are not fixed but emergent. Care, in this context, is the tension required to hold the field open long enough for something new to become real.
