# Feedback-Recomputed Re-entry Slice

## Bounded claim

This slice allows a projection produced by a feedback-grounded field revision
to re-enter diffraction as a current structural witness and to receive later
observed effects. Authority comes from exact current correspondence, retained
feedback-revision lineage, and an active local counter-cut. It does not come
from revision depth, recency, prior confirmation, or repeated correspondence.

Later observations may confirm, complicate, or invalidate the recomputed
formation. An earlier confirmation remains a situated record of correspondence
with the trace observed at that time. A later complication or invalidation is
appended as another situated effect record; it neither overwrites the earlier
confirmation nor converts it into error, confidence loss, or retroactive
failure.

## Re-entry boundary

A feedback-recomputed projection may act as a diffractive witness only when:

- it is the exact current retained projection;
- exactly one retained feedback revision produced that trace;
- the revision retains its original disturbing feedback record;
- the observation, disturbance delta, and substrate invalidation agree;
- the counter-cut preserves the reopened formation and observation lineage;
- the field explicitly identifies that active counter-cut as the projection's
  enabling formation.

The later consequential difference must still satisfy the ordinary diffraction
requirements. Recomputed status does not make equal effects consequential.

## Later-effect boundary

Effects may return against the exact feedback-recomputed trace while its
enabling counter-cut remains active. Confirmation records correspondence and
does not mutate the substrate. Complication reopens only a caller-named active
dependent formation. Invalidation may reopen the feedback-grounded counter-cut
and its dependent closure.

Distinct observations remain distinct records. Their order and number are
historical facts, not weights. No earlier confirmation protects the formation,
and no later disturbance condemns the earlier correspondence or the revised
projection.

## Adversarial boundaries

- Confirmation records cannot substitute for missing recomputation lineage.
- Confirmation count creates no re-entry authority.
- Forged reopened-cut, observation, delta, or counter-cut lineage fails.
- An inactive feedback counter-cut removes current witness authority.
- Equal effects cannot masquerade as a consequential difference.
- Later complication does not silently become enabling-cut invalidation.
- Later invalidation remains a local reopening rather than failure.
- Rejected operations leave substrate state unchanged.

The operative records contain no confidence, weight, count, score, rank,
preference, winner, success, failure, finality, or retroactive-failure field.

## Deliberate exclusions

This slice adds no model, API, renderer, persistence integration, public
articulation, confidence accumulator, chooser, or automatic feedback response.
It does not yet revise and recompute the feedback-grounded formation after a
second-round complication or invalidation.

The next bounded pass should allow later complicating or invalidating effects
on a feedback-recomputed projection to ground another local counter-cut and
fresh recomputation, while preserving the entire correspondence and disturbance
history without allowing feedback depth to become authority.
