# Competing Arrangements and Harm-Constraint Slice

## Status

This is a bounded executable extension of the local-cut and arrangement
variation substrates. It retains several structurally distinct proposed
arrangements, traces each arrangement across the five recorded structural
dimensions, and records when an arrangement crosses an explicit local harm
constraint.

It does not select an arrangement, rank alternatives, calculate utility,
recognize care, certify play, perform diffraction, or produce a public
intervention.

## Operative boundary

`retain_competing_alternatives` receives one enacted source cut, two or more
proposed replacement cuts, and zero or more explicit `HarmConstraint`
records. It:

- requires every proposal to be materially different from the source;
- requires at least two structurally distinct alternatives;
- rejects duplicate allocation topology even when cut IDs, position names,
  relations, or compassionate wording differ;
- preserves local-position correspondence rather than allowing renamed keys
  to manufacture multiplicity;
- traces burden, capacity, consequence, power, and possibility independently;
- records changes in total allocation, maximum local load, occupied local
  positions, and distribution;
- evaluates each named constraint independently; and
- retains input order and every harm-crossing alternative as trace evidence.

The returned `CompetingAlternatives` record has no score, rank, winner,
preferred alternative, selection, or choice operation.

## What a harm trace means

The numeric substrate cannot establish a universal meaning of harm. A
`HarmConstraint` is an accountable local boundary supplied to this
mechanism. It identifies:

- a stable constraint ID;
- one structural dimension;
- one observable measure; and
- whether an increase or decrease in that measure is prohibited.

For example, the Case 2 fixture can state separately that a variation must
not increase total consequence, must not further concentrate maximum burden,
and must not reduce recorded possibility. If a proposal crosses one of
those boundaries, the trace records the exact before and after values. The
breaches are never summed. Passing one constraint does not compensate for
crossing another.

A constraint breach does not erase the proposal from the trace and does not
prove that every possible enactment of a similar topology is harmful. It
records why this locally proposed variation is not materially available
under the supplied boundary. The constraint, its provenance, and its future
revision remain responsibilities of a semantic apparatus that this slice
does not implement.

## Case 2 probe

The active Case 2 cut begins with four burden units concentrated at the
disclosing position after the overwhelm disclosure has already invalidated
the individualized-failure cut.

The competing field retains at least these two possibilities:

- two units remain at the disclosing position and two move to the
  coordination arrangement; and
- one unit remains at the disclosing position while the remaining three are
  distributed across coordination and affected-work positions.

Both preserve the recorded capacity, consequence, power, and possibility
allocations. Neither is called caring or correct. A third probe that removes
recorded possibility or increases consequence remains visible but carries
the exact constraint breach. Paraphrased deadline demands do not enter the
alternative field because transport wording cannot create a structural
variation.

## Falsification boundaries

The focused tests require that:

- one proposal cannot be called competing variation;
- duplicate or compassionately relabeled topology cannot count as another
  alternative;
- renamed local keys cannot evade correspondence checks;
- every retained alternative traces all five structural dimensions;
- a harm-crossing alternative remains in the trace;
- harm is not inferred when no explicit constraint establishes the boundary;
- independent constraint breaches never become a net score;
- harm traces do not reorder the alternatives;
- paraphrased transport demands do not become arrangement proposals; and
- existing transport records and substrate deltas remain unchanged.

## What remains unimplemented

This slice does not originate alternatives from language or material traces.
It does not establish where harm constraints come from, whose capacity or
vulnerability becomes perceptible, or how a constraint is revised when a
new relation enters. It does not diffract the alternatives through one
another, enact one, observe its effects, or reinsert those effects into the
continuing configuration.

The next valid pass should not add a winner. It should make differences among
the retained arrangements disturb one another: a consequence, power, or
capacity trace in one arrangement must be able to revise the cuts that made
another arrangement appear possible. That pass must still stop before model
generation or public articulation.
