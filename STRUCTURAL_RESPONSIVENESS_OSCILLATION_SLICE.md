# Structural Responsiveness and Oscillation Slice

## Bounded claim

This slice can audit a historical sequence of arrangement traces and
distinguish material responsiveness from unsupported structural recurrence.
It does not stop recursion, select an arrangement, infer an optimum, or treat
convergence, persistence, recency, revision depth, or repetition count as
authority.

## Operative distinction

The audit retains four non-ranked findings:

- **Material change:** the current allocation topology differs from the
  immediately prior topology and does not recreate an earlier recorded state.
- **Material recurrence:** a recorded topology recurs while a newly operative
  transport disturbance, enabling formation, or explicit harm-boundary trace
  has changed.
- **Structural oscillation:** a prior topology returns after departure without
  any such newly operative material difference.
- **Structural persistence:** the immediately prior topology remains and no new
  operative material is recorded.

These are descriptions of the trace, not valuations. Persistence is not
success. Oscillation is not automatic failure. Recurrence does not become
preferable because it is recent, frequent, deep in a revision genealogy, or
the last state examined.

## Evidence boundary

Each historical witness must identify:

- an exact traced arrangement;
- its recorded enabling formations;
- the recorded substrate deltas operative for the witness; and
- the transport traces carried by those deltas.

The audit rejects unknown enabling cuts, unknown deltas, duplicate witness
authority, and transport claims that do not exactly match their recorded
deltas. Recorded but inactive cuts remain usable as historical evidence.

## Adversarial properties

- A-B-A without new material is recorded as oscillation.
- A-B-A with a recorded new disturbance or changed formation remains material
  recurrence rather than oscillation.
- An unchanged topology can remain materially responsive when a recorded new
  disturbance becomes operative.
- Unchanged topology without new material is persistence, not convergence.
- Repeating a cycle more times does not change the finding or add authority.
- Invented transport novelty cannot hide oscillation.
- The audit exposes no score, rank, winner, preferred state, stopping method,
  terminal flag, convergence threshold, or iteration limit.

## Deliberate exclusions

This slice does not decide whether continued responsiveness is caring, whether
an oscillation should be interrupted, or whether persistence is warranted. It
does not originate a counter-cut, enact a public intervention, observe external
effects, call a model, render language, or persist application state.

The next architectural boundary is to make an oscillation trace itself
available as material for a local counter-cut without converting its detection
into a command to stop or settle.
