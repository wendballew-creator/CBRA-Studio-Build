# Case Study 001: Noticing, Correction, and Diffractive Inquiry

The first case study built from real arriving material: an encounter
between the author and another AI assistant on 6 November 2026, exported
from that conversation as a research archive and brought into this
repository the same day.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `source_excerpts.md` | Seventeen verbatim excerpts with timestamps, speakers and analyst tags, with the dossier's own integrity note |
| `selected_verbatim_excerpts.csv`, `.json` | The same excerpts as data |
| `analysis_and_protocol.md` | The working analysis (v0.1): research question, encounter sequence, diffractive reading, six candidate requirements, a three-condition test protocol with six evaluation criteria |
| `../../measurement/case_001_probes.md` | The encounter as a probe set for the measurement run |

## Provenance

- Source of record: an SRT transcript of a voice conversation, held by the
  author outside this repository. Its timestamps are compressed and are not
  elapsed time.
- Archive: `CBRA_Case_Study_001_Initial_Archive.zip`, produced in the
  originating chat on 6 November 2026, containing two Word documents and the
  excerpts as CSV and JSON. The original archive is kept by the author; a
  copy sits in the repository's ignored `exports/session-documents/`.
- Conversion: the Word documents were converted to Markdown on 6 November
  2026 without change of wording except the redactions below. The page
  thumbnail and the creator fields were removed from the author's cleaned
  Word copies.

## Redaction log

Two personal names were replaced with bracketed placeholders, in every copy
of the excerpts (Markdown, CSV, JSON, and the cleaned Word documents):

| Excerpt | Replacement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 03 (assistant, 00:00:12.600) | `[a friend]` | a person in the author's life, named in a relational account |
| 11 (user, 00:00:18.000) | `[a colleague]` | a person whose writing the author discusses; not a cited scholar |

Nothing else was changed. The excerpts the probes use (01, 04, 06, 08, 10)
contained no personal names. If the second person is in fact a published
scholar cited as such, restore the name: the author's rule is that cited
scholars stay.

## Evidence boundary

The excerpts are selected, not exhaustive. Nothing here recovers the other
assistant's internals; "safety heuristic" is the author's inference. What
the case study supports is a comparison: the same arrivals through a
baseline, through CBRA's instructions alone, and through the method with
its constraints, read against the six criteria in the protocol.
