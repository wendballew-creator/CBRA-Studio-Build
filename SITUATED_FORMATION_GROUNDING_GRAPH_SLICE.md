# Situated Formation Grounding Graph Slice

Date: 2026-08-04
Base commit: `b22ba66`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose

The first situated-formation boundary required exact arrival evidence and
named apparatus participation, but it did not require every supplied noticing
or apparatus account to participate in the enacted cut. That gap allowed a
caller to surround a grounded position with valid but ornamental records.
Apparent plurality could therefore remain documentary rather than operative.

This slice closes the grounding graph without treating multiplicity as
confirmation, weight, or authority.

## Operative relation

`SituatedFormationBoundary` now requires:

- every situated noticing to ground at least one enacted local position;
- every apparatus participation to participate in at least one grounded
  noticing;
- position-to-noticing, noticing-to-apparatus, evidence, and apparatus
  transport references to be unique within their local relation;
- the existing exact equality between noticing evidence and the cut transport
  basis.

A proposal containing an ornamental noticing, unused apparatus participation,
or duplicated grounding/evidence link fails before substrate mutation.

Several grounded noticings may participate in the same position. Reversing
their record order or grounding-link order produces the same cut, delta, and
configuration. Their number is not measured and confers no confidence,
seniority, adequacy, truth, or selection authority.

## What remains external

The caller still supplies every description and semantic proposal. The
runtime now validates that the proposal forms a materially connected graph;
it does not determine that the graph is the right account of an arrival.
Exact evidence establishes traceability, and connected participation
establishes operativity, not truth.

The situated formation records are validated at enactment but are not yet
retained as durable runtime state. Durable, lossless retention belongs to the
later continuity and persistence milestone; it must not be smuggled into this
formation slice as documentary authority.

## Bounded claim

A situated cut can no longer be enacted with decorative apparatus or noticing
records. Every supplied formation element must participate in the local
grounding graph, while plurality and order remain non-authoritative.
