# Dependency-Activated Origination Slice

Date: 2026-08-13
Base commit: `68edd60`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose

Recorded proposal emissions previously entered an assembly through direct
calls with no operative account of why those proposal-generating
participations became active. Adding a universal generation stage would
restore a pipeline. Allowing one opaque model call to generate the proposal
field would restore a semantic former.

This slice lets arrival-grounded unresolved relations open materially plural
participation invitations. Proposal emissions answer invitations
asynchronously; activation does not prescribe a stage order or make an
invitation semantically authoritative.

## Operative relation

`UnresolvedArrivalRelation` requires exact arrival evidence, an account of the
local unresolved difference, exclusions, and revision conditions.

`ProposalParticipationInvitation` binds one or more unresolved relations to a
specific apparatus participation and records what difference it may open, its
limits, and the conditions that deactivate the invitation. Every unresolved
relation must activate at least one invitation. Every invitation must arise
from the exact evidence of its named unresolved relations, and all invitations
in one activation field must be materially distinct.

`DependencyActivatedOriginationAssembly` retains proposal emissions only when
they:

- answer an active invitation;
- use exactly the invited apparatus participation;
- remain grounded in the unresolved arrival basis;
- pass the accountable situated-formation and origin/check boundaries; and
- use an emission transport not answering another invitation.

Contribution and proposal identifiers remain unique across answered
invitations. A collision is rejected during recording rather than retained as
an enactment-time failure.

At least two materially distinct answered invitations are required for plural
enactment. The assembly derives and enacts the plural proposal field through
the previously established recorded-origination boundary.

## No fixed pipeline

Invitations do not create a required sequence. Several may be active together,
and responses may arrive in any order. An unanswered invitation does not block
enactment once materially plural participation has occurred; it remains
visible as ungenerated participation. Invitation order and answer order produce
the same configuration.

`OriginationActivationTrace` exposes only the field, relation, invitation,
answered/unanswered, and revision-condition identifiers needed to inspect this
boundary. Answered is a factual participation record, not acceptance or
adequacy.

No priority, next-stage, rank, score, preferred invitation, winning proposal,
or completion state exists.

## Model apparatus boundary

A transport marked `model-output` must expose provider, model, effective
configuration, prompt dependencies, source dependencies, known limits, and an
explicit human-centered-training-apparatus mark. Separate calls are not
presumed independent. Their shared and differing apparatus conditions remain
transport provenance and participation material.

The same emission transport cannot answer multiple invitations, and one
answered invitation cannot enact a semantic field. A model proposal still
cannot check itself because every retained proposal must satisfy the existing
materially distinct origin/check boundary.

## Remaining external formation

The runtime validates an unresolved-relation activation; it does not infer
semantic unresolvedness directly from opaque bytes. Callers still compose the
descriptions, invitations, and proposal content. Exact evidence establishes
traceability, not the truth of those descriptions.

This slice therefore displaces the universal generation stage and opaque
single-call reasoner without claiming autonomous semantic understanding.
Unresolved-relation and invitation records remain runtime-local and are not
yet durably retained or recursively invalidated after enactment.

## Bounded claim

Arrival-grounded unresolved relations can now activate multiple materially
distinct proposal-generating participations without imposing a universal
formation sequence. Asynchronous, separately provenanced emissions may answer
those invitations, while one invitation or model emission cannot constitute,
validate, or complete the proposal field.
