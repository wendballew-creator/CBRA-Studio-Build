# Plural Relation-to-Material Activation Slice

Status date: 2026-08-18

This slice removes the caller-supplied semantic shortcut from formation
reopening. A reopening call no longer names the later transports and relations
whose connection should count as disturbance. That connection must first be
formed through separately recorded, materially plural apparatus participation.

## Recorded activation contributions

Each `RelationMaterialActivationContribution` exposes:

- one accountable apparatus participation;
- exact spans of already recorded later-material transports;
- the unresolved relations encountered through that participation;
- differences made perceptible in that relation-to-material encounter;
- exclusions; and
- revision conditions.

The participation's transport basis must exactly equal its contribution's
later-material evidence. Original arrival evidence cannot be reused as later
disturbance, and an unknown or out-of-bounds span cannot enter the field.

`InspectableReopeningActivationAssembly.record(...)` accepts one contribution
at a time. Contributions remain inspectable before enactment. One
participation identifier cannot be rebound across contributions, and
materially equivalent apparatus signatures cannot simulate plurality.

## Derived reopening

The activation assembly requires at least two materially distinct
contributions. More strongly, every relation that the field proposes to reopen
must appear through at least two distinct apparatus participations. The field
therefore cannot treat an unrelated second contribution as plural
certification of a relation formed by only one account.

`enact(...)` accepts only a delta identifier. It derives triggering transports,
reopened relations, and activation contribution identifiers from the recorded
field, then delegates recursive invalidation to the situated formation and
substrate. Contribution order confers no authority, and no final caller or
single model emission supplies the complete semantic field.

The resulting `FormationReopeningTrace` retains the activation field,
contribution identifiers, unavailable or ungenerated possibilities, revision
conditions, and mechanically derived invalidation consequences. It contains
no winner, rank, score, confidence, correctness, maturity, acceptance, or
failure state.

## Remaining boundary

This is a plural inspectability boundary, not autonomous semantic discovery.
Each contribution still arrives as structured material supplied to the
runtime, and the runtime cannot prove that two declared apparatus accounts
have different hidden authors. Executable activation participants, durable
persistence, public intervention reinsertion, and behavioral falsification
remain later work.

The tests establish only these bounded mechanics. They do not certify that an
activation is paradigmatically valid or ontologically complete.
