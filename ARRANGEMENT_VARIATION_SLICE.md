# Arrangement Variation Slice

## Status

This is a bounded executable extension of the local-cut substrate. It does
not implement semantic formation, recognize care, certify play, or produce a
public response.

The slice adds one capability: after a semantic apparatus has enacted an
active local cut and identified a concentrated burden, the runtime can
construct an alternative burden allocation and compare it mechanically with
the active arrangement.

## Operative boundary

`ArrangementExplorer` receives cut-local references. It does not read or
classify transport payloads. It can:

- refuse a source that is not active;
- refuse a source position or support position outside the local cut;
- refuse a distribution that is not structurally concentrated;
- retain a bounded share of burden at the source position;
- distribute the remainder across selected local support positions;
- preserve capacity, consequence, power, possibility, relations, revision
  conditions, and exclusions in the generated candidate;
- compare before/after allocation shapes without treating new IDs, renamed
  positions, or relation wording as material difference; and
- submit the candidate through the existing atomic disturbance boundary so
  invalidation still propagates to dependent cuts.

The object returned is named `BurdenRedistributionCandidate`. It contains a
replacement cut and a derived comparison. It has no `care`, `play`, score,
rationale, or approval field. A candidate may provide evidence relevant to a
later care or play determination; it cannot make that determination itself.

## Case 2 probe

The Case 2 fixture begins after the overwhelm disclosure has disturbed the
individual-failure account. One local position still carries all four burden
units. The candidate retains two units there and moves two units to the
coordination arrangement. Total burden and the existing capacity,
consequence, power, and possibility allocations remain intact.

When enacted, the alternative invalidates the source cut and a dependent
prospective demand. The delta reports a changed burden distribution. Merely
adding a paraphrased demand, changing position or relation labels, or removing
the candidate produces no structural variation.

This is not evidence that equal sharing is universally caring. The selection
of positions and the retained fraction are accountable cuts supplied to the
mechanism. Their effects remain open to further disturbance.

## Falsification boundaries

The focused probes require that:

- a component or field cannot declare that care occurred;
- removing the alternative removes the structural difference;
- relabeling an identical allocation cannot pass as variation;
- empathy or supportive relation wording cannot pass as variation;
- non-local support and non-concentrated burden fail closed;
- consequence erasure remains visible rather than being mistaken for care;
- transport payloads remain unchanged and uninterpreted; and
- the generated candidate changes burden topology while preserving all other
  recorded structural dimensions.

## What remains unimplemented

This slice does not determine which local positions should share burden, how
power should change, whether a proposed redistribution is desirable, or what
sequence, pace, access, support, timing, boundary, or scale should change. It
does not yet enact several competing arrangements and follow their effects.
It also does not implement diffraction across apparatus and material
relations, public intervention, effect reinsertion, persistence, a model,
API, browser, or live UX.

The next valid pass must not jump to language generation. It should add a
constraint-driven arrangement set that can retain multiple alternatives,
record why variation is unavailable when it would increase harm, and trace
each alternative's effects without selecting one through a hidden score.
