# Post-Return Effect Feedback Slice

## Bounded claim

This slice allows effects observed after an oscillation-grounded field return
to re-enter the current recursive field as feedback.  The observation is bound
to one exact, currently retained recomputation and its active enabling
counter-cut.  Feedback may be taken up locally as confirmation, complication,
or invalidation without making any of those dispositions a score, judgment,
selection, stopping rule, or terminal state.

## Operative boundary

- Confirmation requires correspondence across every supplied observed
  dimension.  It appends an audit trace to the field but does not mutate the
  substrate, protect the enabling cut, or mark the formation complete.
- Complication requires a recorded structural difference and an explicitly
  named active formation that depends on the enabling counter-cut.  It reopens
  that dependent closure while leaving the enabling counter-cut active.
- Invalidation requires a recorded structural difference and explicitly names
  the enabling counter-cut as its local root.  The resulting substrate delta
  recursively invalidates its active dependents.  The disposition records the
  scope of uptake; it does not describe the recomputation as a failure.

No mismatch automatically selects its own scope.  A caller must propose the
local uptake, and the transaction validates that proposal against the retained
field and substrate before mutation.

## Preserved evidence

Each feedback trace retains:

- the exact post-return observation transport ID;
- the exact current recomputed structural trace;
- observed measures for each supplied structural dimension;
- the dimensions that differ from the recomputation;
- the explicitly proposed local disposition;
- the affected cut, when a disturbance is enacted; and
- the exact derived substrate delta, when one exists.

Feedback history remains part of the recursively revised field and survives
later recursive re-entry.  One observation cannot be reused to manufacture
repetition, weight, confidence, or authority.

## Adversarial boundary

The tests establish that:

- unrecorded observations cannot enter;
- forged or stale field-recomputation lineage has no feedback authority;
- confirmation cannot carry a mutation or protection request;
- structural correspondence cannot authorize complication or invalidation;
- structural difference does not automatically choose an affected formation;
- complication cannot silently become enabling-cut invalidation;
- invalidation cannot target an unrelated cut;
- the same observation cannot be replayed as fresh feedback;
- rejected transactions leave substrate state unchanged; and
- feedback records contain no score, rank, winner, preference, success,
  failure, finality, iteration, or confidence field.

## Explicit exclusions

This slice does not observe a live world, infer structural measures from
ordinary language, determine whether an intervention is caring, select an
arrangement, generate or render a public response, integrate persistence, or
declare a formation verified.  It establishes only the local feedback
re-entry mechanics after a field return.
