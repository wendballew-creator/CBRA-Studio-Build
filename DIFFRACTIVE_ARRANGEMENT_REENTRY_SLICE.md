# Diffractive Arrangement Re-entry Slice

## Status

This is a bounded executable extension of the competing-arrangements slice.
It lets a measured structural difference in one retained arrangement reopen
an explicit active cut through which another arrangement became available.
The substrate then recursively invalidates every active cut that depended on
that enabling cut.

It does not rank or select arrangements, infer the meaning of a difference,
certify diffraction, originate arrangements, or form a public intervention.

## Operative boundary

`DiffractiveArrangementField` retains the existing non-ranked competing field
and adds one `ArrangementDependency` record for every alternative. The record
identifies the active local cuts through which that proposed arrangement
became available. It does not turn the proposal into a globally stable entity.

`disturb_through_arrangement_difference` requires:

- two different retained arrangements;
- an explicit structural dimension and inspectable effect measure;
- unequal measured effects after the two proposed arrangements;
- a target cut already recorded as enabling the contrasted arrangement;
- transport evidence already present in the substrate; and
- an invalidation path containing the enabling cut and at least one active
  dependent cut.

The operation records the two measured values and calls the existing atomic
disturbance boundary. The contrasted arrangement remains in the retained
field as reopened evidence; it is not silently deleted. Unaffected
alternatives also remain visible. A reopened arrangement cannot become a new
witness until some later mechanism revises its enabling formation.

## Case 2 probe

The Case 2 field retains two burden-sharing proposals. The first leaves a
maximum local burden of `2.0`; the second leaves a maximum local burden of
`1.5`. The difference is not interpreted as proof that the second proposal is
better or more caring.

When the `1.5` trace is allowed to disturb the cuts that made the `2.0`
proposal available, the substrate reopens:

- the shared-arrangement availability cut;
- its dependent actionability cut; and
- its dependent release-condition cut.

The distributed-arrangement availability and actionability cuts remain
active. Both proposals remain in the competing record. What changed is not
their order or score; the prior conditions under which one proposal appeared
available can no longer remain active without revision.

## Falsification boundaries

The focused tests require that:

- equal consequence traces cannot be called a consequential difference;
- prose, local names, relation labels, or compassionate wording cannot create
  a difference;
- an alternative cannot reopen a cut absent from its explicit dependency
  record;
- invalidating only one isolated cut cannot be called a diffractive movement;
- every retained alternative has exactly one dependency record;
- recursive invalidation reaches multiple dependent parts;
- the reopened alternative remains as evidence rather than disappearing;
- the field contains no score, rank, winner, preferred option, or chooser;
- transport paraphrases alone produce no arrangement disturbance; and
- a reopened arrangement cannot provide a stale difference before revision.

Every rejected operation leaves the substrate unchanged.

## Claim limit

This slice establishes one required property of a diffractive movement: a
difference traced in one proposed arrangement can revise the active cuts that
made another proposal appear possible, and the effects propagate through
dependencies.

It does not establish that the comparison axis was the right one, that the
dependency account is semantically adequate, that the resulting active field
is caring, or that a reopened proposal has been revised. Those remain future
semantic and recursive responsibilities.

The next valid pass should support an explicit counter-cut or replacement for
the reopened enabling formation and then recompute the affected arrangement's
effect trace. It must preserve the disturbance history and must not convert
recursive revision into ranking or selection. Model generation and public
articulation remain outside the boundary.
