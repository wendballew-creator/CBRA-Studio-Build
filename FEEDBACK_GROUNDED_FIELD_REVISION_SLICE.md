# Feedback-Grounded Field Revision Slice

## Bounded claim

This slice allows one exact retained post-return feedback trace with a
`COMPLICATES` or `INVALIDATES` disposition to become material for a proposed
local counter-cut and a freshly recomputed competing-field projection.
Feedback does not mutate the field automatically. The caller must name the
retained observation, propose the counter-cut, and supply the recomputed
arrangement.

Confirming feedback remains in the recursive field as revisable evidence. It
does not authorize a counter-cut, protect a formation, accumulate confidence,
or create a presumption in favor of the current projection.

## Transaction boundary

The feedback-revision transaction requires exact agreement among:

- one retained feedback trace;
- the observation transport recorded by the substrate;
- the feedback disturbance delta recorded by the substrate;
- the local cut identified and invalidated by that delta;
- the current retained projection and the trace originally observed;
- the current field dependency through which that projection was available;
- the proposed counter-cut's observation, source, and reopened-cut lineage;
- the recomputed proposal's prior-proposal and counter-cut dependencies.

Complication and invalidation retain different local scopes. A complication
may ground its counter-cut in the explicitly reopened dependent formation
while the earlier enabling counter-cut remains active. An invalidation may
ground its counter-cut in the reopened enabling formation itself. Neither
disposition is treated as failure.

The complete competing field is retraced before the counter-cut is enacted.
This preflight rejects cosmetic recomputation, duplicate topology, lost local
correspondence, and inconsistent lineage before substrate mutation.

## Preserved history

`FeedbackFieldRevisionTrace` retains:

- the complete feedback trace and observed effects;
- the exact reopened local formation;
- the enacted counter-cut;
- the displaced current proposal and structural trace;
- the newly recomputed proposal and structural trace.

The field also retains all earlier confirmation, complication, invalidation,
oscillation, counter-cut, recomputation, and diffractive re-entry records.
Only the current projection is substituted. Unaffected competing alternatives
remain present.

## Adversarial boundaries

- Confirmation has no counter-cut authority.
- Multiple or earlier confirmations create no weight or confidence.
- A forged or unrecorded feedback delta has no revision authority.
- Feedback cannot be detached from its observation provenance.
- An unrelated or relabeled formation cannot become the local counter-cut.
- A cosmetic recomputation cannot re-enter under a new identifier.
- A duplicate arrangement topology cannot re-enter as fresh multiplicity.
- One feedback trace cannot authorize repeated field revisions.
- Rejected transactions leave substrate state unchanged.

The operative records contain no score, confidence, weight, rank, preference,
winner, success, failure, finality, stopping, or selection field.

## Deliberate exclusions

This slice does not add a model, API, renderer, persistence integration,
public articulation, score, chooser, confidence accumulator, or automatic
feedback response. It does not yet let the feedback-recomputed projection act
as a fresh diffractive witness or let later observations revise the meaning of
an earlier confirmation.

The next bounded pass should allow a feedback-recomputed projection to re-enter
diffraction and receive later effects, including effects that complicate an
earlier confirmation, without turning a history of correspondence into
confidence or a later disturbance into retroactive failure.
