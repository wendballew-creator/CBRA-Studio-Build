# Recursive Diffractive Re-entry Slice

## Boundary

This pass begins after a disturbed arrangement has received an explicit
counter-cut and a freshly recomputed structural trace. It adds the smallest
mechanism that can:

- let that recomputed arrangement become a diffractive witness;
- disturb another active enabling formation, including a counter-cut enacted
  during an earlier revision;
- preserve every disturbance, revision, invalidated cut, and recomputed trace;
  and
- prevent revision depth, recency, history position, or repetition from
  becoming ranking authority.

It does not infer a consequential difference from language, rank revisions,
select an arrangement, certify care, enact a public intervention, or observe
an external effect.

## Operative movement

The Case 2 fixture now completes a recursive sequence:

1. The distributed proposal disturbs the shared proposal's enabling
   formation.
2. An explicit shared counter-cut enables a recomputed shared arrangement.
3. A capacity difference in that recomputed arrangement disturbs the
   distributed proposal's enabling formation.
4. An explicit distributed counter-cut enables a recomputed distributed
   arrangement.
5. A power difference in the recomputed distributed arrangement re-enters and
   disturbs the earlier shared counter-cut and its active dependent.

The earlier counter-cut is not protected by having already served as a
revision. It remains a local, revisable formation. Its invalidation does not
erase the disturbance and revision history through which it became active.

## Re-entry authority

A recomputed arrangement may re-enter only when all of the following agree:

- its cut is currently retained in the competing field;
- its complete traced structure exactly matches one recorded recomputation;
- its explicit counter-cut is still an active enabling formation;
- the recomputed proposal preserves lineage to the reopened proposal;
- the counter-cut preserves lineage to the invalidated enabling cut;
- the substrate records that cut as invalidated by the same disturbance delta;
  and
- the retained disturbance and substrate delta are exact matches.

No timestamp, revision number, generation, depth, or iteration count
participates in this validation. Reversing the revision-history tuple leaves
the operation unchanged. Repeating the same revision record is rejected
rather than treated as added evidence or weight.

## History preservation

`RecursiveDiffractiveReentryTrace` records the current recomputed witness, the
counter-cut through which it remains available, the target enabling cut, and
the complete disturbance trace. `RecursivelyRevisedArrangementField`
preserves these re-entry records alongside every prior revision.

The substrate remains the independent structural record. Invalidated cuts
remain stored with the delta that invalidated them, and all transport records
remain unchanged. Current activity and historical inscription therefore stay
distinct.

## Adversarial boundaries

The focused suite requires that:

- an unrevised alternative cannot claim recursive re-entry authority;
- a recomputed arrangement with an inactive counter-cut cannot act as a stale
  witness;
- a forged revision cannot substitute a different disturbance history;
- duplicated revision records cannot manufacture iteration weight;
- revision tuple order cannot create recency authority;
- an earlier active counter-cut can be disturbed when it explicitly enables
  the contrasted arrangement and has active dependents;
- equal effects, relabeling, and transport paraphrases remain insufficient;
- rejected operations leave substrate state unchanged; and
- no score, rank, winner, preferred flag, chooser, depth, recency, iteration,
  or generation field is exposed.

## Claim limit and next boundary

This slice proves that a recomputed arrangement can re-enter diffraction and
disturb another active formation, including an earlier counter-cut, without
genealogical seniority becoming selection authority.

It does not yet bound recursive reopening against non-termination or
oscillation. The next valid pass should distinguish sustained material
responsiveness from a configuration cycling among previously encountered
structural states, while retaining unresolved incompatibility and without
using convergence, stability, or iteration limits as hidden preference for a
particular arrangement. Model generation, persistence integration, and public
articulation remain outside this boundary.
