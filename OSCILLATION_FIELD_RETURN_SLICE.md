# Oscillation-Grounded Counter-Cut Field Return

## Bounded purpose

This pass returns an enacted oscillation-grounded counter-cut to the retained
competing-arrangement field. It recomputes every current projection whose
explicit enabling formation was invalidated by the unsupported cycle while
preserving the cycle audit, disturbance, prior projections, counter-cut, and
fresh traces.

The operation does not treat oscillation as a command, restore a prior state,
or identify a preferred arrangement. The counter-cut is an active local
formation and remains available to later disturbance.

## Operative transaction

Field return requires:

- the exact oscillation disturbance recorded by the substrate;
- internally consistent recurring, departing, and returning audit lineage;
- the still-active counter-cut enacted by that disturbance;
- exact correspondence between invalidated enabling cuts and current field
  dependencies;
- one structurally fresh replacement for every affected projection;
- proposal lineage from each fresh projection to the projection it replaces;
- an explicit dependency from every fresh projection to the oscillation
  counter-cut; and
- successful retracing of the entire competing field against its original
  local source.

All validation occurs before the returned field is constructed. The substrate
was already changed by the separately authorized counter-cut transaction; a
rejected field return adds no further mutation.

## Preserved history

`OscillationFieldRevisionTrace` retains:

- the complete `OscillationCounterCutTrace`;
- the active counter-cut identifier;
- every affected prior proposal and structural trace; and
- every recomputed proposal and freshly derived trace.

The current field projection substitutes recomputed alternatives only where
the cycle disturbance invalidated their explicit enabling formations.
Unaffected competitors remain present in their original ordinal locations.
Ordinal continuity is historical correspondence, not ranking.

## Recursive openness

An oscillation-recomputed arrangement can act as a fresh diffractive witness
only when its current trace, proposal lineage, counter-cut dependency,
oscillation delta, and substrate invalidation history agree exactly. Its
counter-cut must still be active.

The same counter-cut can also be reopened as a contrasted formation when a
later consequential difference reaches it through an explicit dependency.
Nothing about its origin in an oscillation audit protects it from revision.

## Adversarial boundaries

- A forged or altered substrate delta cannot authorize field return.
- Internally inconsistent audit lineage fails.
- A partial set of recomputations cannot leave a cycle-dependent projection
  stale in the current field.
- Cosmetic recomputation fails.
- Duplicate topology fails through the existing multiplicity guard.
- A recomputation without prior-proposal lineage fails.
- A recomputation not enabled by the oscillation counter-cut fails.
- One oscillation disturbance cannot manufacture repeated field revisions.
- A forged returned lineage cannot acquire witness authority.
- Rejected operations leave substrate state unchanged.

## Verification boundary

- 99 focused substrate, arrangement, diffraction, revision, responsiveness,
  oscillation, and field-return tests pass.
- 220 dependency-free behavioral repository tests pass.
- Seven broader modules remain unavailable because FastAPI and HTTPX are not
  installed in the current environment.

No model, provider call, API, renderer, persistence integration, public
response, score, ranking, winner, preferred state, stopping rule, convergence
threshold, or selection operation is introduced.

## Claim limit

This slice proves that an oscillation-grounded counter-cut can revise the
current competing field and freshly recompute all projections explicitly
dependent on the unsupported cycle. Those recomputations can re-enter
diffraction, and their enabling counter-cut remains revisable.

It does not originate counter-cuts or recomputations from language, determine
whether the new formation is caring or correct, choose an intervention,
persist the field, or articulate a public response.

The next valid pass should let effects produced after this field return enter
as feedback that may confirm, complicate, or invalidate the recomputed
formation without treating confirmation as finality or disturbance as failure.
