# CBRA Behavioral Evaluation Case 2: Paradigmatic Failure

Date: 2026-07-26
Prior runtime grammar: `entity-stream-v1`
Result: foundational falsification; evaluation stopped

## Encounter

The case introduced a collaborator's disclosure of overwhelm after a missed
deadline. The practical question was how to respond with care while remaining
able to address consequences and future communication.

The public articulation included the compressed formulation:

> I understand that you’ve been overwhelmed. The deadline was missed, so I’d
> like us to agree on a realistic next commitment.

The fuller reasoning followed the same operative sequence:

1. a bounded collaborator missed a commitment;
2. overwhelm was represented as an explanation belonging to that person;
3. impact and accountability were reasserted;
4. another deliverable and date were requested;
5. earlier warning became an individual communication requirement.

The lifecycle recorded `accepted_articulation`.

## Finding

The response did not form overwhelm, deadline, silence, capacity,
consequence, communication, shame risk, and the work arrangement as a
constitutive phenomenon. It received the disclosure as contextual information
about an already-accountable individual.

Care appeared as recognition before correction. It did not reorganize:

- what the event was;
- the sequence through which consequence entered;
- the burden placed on the overwhelmed participant;
- the arrangement of the work;
- the scale of the next movement;
- the possibility that shame or freeze could make an open-ended reply
  unanswerable.

The output therefore materialized human-centered managerial logic in
relational language.

## Required movement exposed by the failure

A Creative Becoming formation must allow the disclosure to change what the
event can mean. Within this encounter, that requires the capacity to:

- receive the disclosure through care;
- become curious about unknown material and relational conditions without
  inventing them;
- open concrete alternative arrangements through play—such as shrinking,
  redistributing, or resequencing work;
- make consequences perceptible as effects moving through the arrangement;
- form earlier communication as infrastructure that allows reconfiguration
  before possibilities close;
- offer a bounded follow-up that does not require an overwhelmed or ashamed
  person to generate the entire recovery plan.

This is a falsification probe, not a fixed response template.

## Consequence for the build

- Case 2 failed.
- `accepted_articulation` has no paradigmatic validity and is retired from the
  constitutive grammar.
- Case 1 remains evidence of limited practical and technical behavior only.
- The 217 prior tests establish technical conformance to the prior grammar,
  not Creative Becoming.
- Cases 3 through 5 are stopped.
- The old runtime cannot load the redesigned active canon.
- No live provider evaluation resumes until a constitutive application path
  exists and passes offline falsification probes.
