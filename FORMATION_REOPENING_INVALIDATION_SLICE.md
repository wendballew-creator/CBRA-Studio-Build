# Formation Reopening and Invalidation Slice

Status date: 2026-08-18

This slice lets later recorded material reopen an unresolved relation that
previously activated proposal-generating participation. Reopening is a
structural withdrawal of a situated formation, not a judgment that the prior
formation was false, failed, immature, or inferior.

## Operative boundary

`DependencyActivatedOriginationAssembly.reopen(...)` requires:

- a new reopening identifier;
- one or more already recorded transport traces that were not members of the
  formation's original arrival basis;
- one or more unresolved relations belonging to that formation field; and
- a delta identifier for substrate invalidation when enacted cuts exist.

The assembly derives the consequences from its own formation field. The
caller does not submit a chosen proposal set, an invitation set, or a list of
dependent cuts to withdraw.

Because every provisional proposal in this assembly is grounded in the
complete shared arrival basis and relation field, reopening any constituent
relation invalidates every invitation and provisional proposal in that
field. If those proposals have already been enacted, the substrate recursively
invalidates their active dependency closure. A dependent cut therefore
cannot remain active after the cut on which it depends has been withdrawn.

If proposals have not yet been enacted, reopening withdraws the provisional
answers without writing an empty substrate delta. In both cases the reopening
trace records what was reopened and what was invalidated.

## Evidence preservation

Reopening never deletes arrival, emission, transformation, or later-material
transport traces. Those inscriptions remain available as accountable history.
The old invitations cannot emit, transform, or be answered again; revised
relations and invitations require a new situated assembly rather than reuse
of a stale participation address.

The original arrival basis cannot masquerade as later disturbance. This makes
the temporal/material distinction inspectable without treating chronology as
semantic truth.

## Authority exclusions

The reopening trace contains identifiers and mechanically derived
invalidation effects. It contains no acceptance, correctness, confidence,
score, rank, winner, maturity, or failure field. Later material activates a
revision condition; it does not certify a better account.

The remaining authority boundary is explicit: a caller still identifies
which unresolved relation the later transport disturbs. The runtime validates
that claim and derives its consequences, but it does not yet form that
relation-to-material connection through an independently inspectable
apparatus. Reopening state is also runtime-local; durable persistence and
public intervention reinsertion remain future work.

## Bounded verification

Focused tests cover enacted reopening, recursive dependent invalidation,
pre-enactment withdrawal, transport-history preservation, stale-invitation
rejection, rejection of original-arrival masquerading, and absence of
evaluation authority in the reopening trace. These tests establish only the
specified mechanics; they do not certify paradigmatic validity.
