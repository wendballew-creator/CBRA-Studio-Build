# Recorded Plural Origination Slice

Date: 2026-08-13
Base commit: `78df422`
Active grammar: `recursive-reconfiguration-v1`

## Purpose

The inspectable origination field still arrived at enactment as one
caller-composed package containing the field, every proposal, and every
proposal-origin relation. Its records exposed declared control, but the final
caller could still construct or prune the complete submitted field at the
moment of activation.

This slice moves proposal origination before field enactment. Separately
recorded transport emissions now contribute proposals to an assembly, and the
assembly derives the plural field from those prior contributions.

## Operative relation

`RecordedProposalContribution` binds:

- one non-rebindable contribution identifier;
- one accountable situated proposal;
- the exact originating apparatus participation already present in that
  proposal;
- a nonempty exact byte span from a recorded proposal-emission transport; and
- the generative difference contributed by that emission.

The arrival supporting the situated cut cannot also masquerade as its proposal
emission. One recorded emission transport cannot originate multiple proposals
inside the assembly. A contribution is retained only after its exact evidence,
situated grounding graph, and origin/check separation validate.

`RecordedPluralOriginationAssembly.enact()` accepts only a delta identifier.
It derives the proposal set, originating participation, proposal-origin graph,
and generative-difference accounts from contributions already recorded. It
then submits that derived field through the inspectable origination boundary
and the atomic plural-formation boundary.

## Anti-packaging boundary

The final enactment caller cannot supply, remove, reorder, or replace a
proposal or field record. Contribution order produces the same configuration.
Contribution identifiers cannot be rebound, one emission cannot be reused to
generate multiple proposals, and fewer than two separately recorded
contributions cannot enact plurality.

This is not a claim that transport provenance establishes semantic truth. The
emission span shows what material proposed a cut; it does not certify that the
cut adequately describes the arrival.

## Remaining external participation

An external caller still records transport traces and constructs the semantic
content of each proposal contribution. The runtime cannot yet prove that
separate emissions came from independently controlled providers, people, or
material processes. A single caller could invoke the recording operation
several times, and undeclared possibilities remain undetectable.

The change is narrower and operative: final field packaging is no longer an
argument to enactment, proposal emissions are stable transport material rather
than hidden generation, and each retained contribution has already crossed
the accountable situated-formation boundary.

## Persistence boundary

The assembly retains contributions only in its current runtime instance. They
are not yet part of `ConfigurationTrace` and do not survive restart. Durable
retention remains deferred to the continuity and persistence milestone.

## Bounded claim

A plural situated field can now be derived and enacted from separately
recorded, exact, non-rebindable proposal emissions. The final caller cannot
construct the complete proposal field at enactment, while emission provenance
remains evidence of origination rather than confidence, truth, selection, or
certification.
